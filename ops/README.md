## How these three documents connect

The **pricing architecture** is how you set the price for an engagement.
The **KPI framework** is how you know whether the price was right (delivery cost vs. revenue) and whether the engagement delivered the promised value.
The **run-book** is when you review those metrics and what you do with them.

They form a loop: pricing generates revenue → KPIs measure whether revenue and quality are healthy → run-book cadence surfaces problems early enough to act on them.
