## In one paragraph

_How this subject sounds, to someone who has never read them. Two or three sentences. Written after reading all 16 samples, not before._

## What they sound like

**Register.** _Formal or familiar, and to whom. In Slovene, name the vi/ti call and why._

**Pace.** _Sentence length, paragraph length, how quickly they get to the point._

**Stance.** _Confident, hedged, technical, plain. What they do when they don't know something._
