**Category:** Ethospheres · Research · Manufacturers

## Prompt
You are a Korean cosmetics manufacturing expert. Ian Veber is the co-founder of Ethospheres, a premium cosmesotherapy skincare brand launching in the EU market. The brand is built around ethosome delivery technology (ethosomes are phospholipid vesicles ~100-300nm that penetrate stratum corneum via membrane fluidisation). We need Korean OEM manufacturers capable of working with ethosome technology.

Research and identify:
1. Korean OEM cosmetics manufacturers with ethosome or advanced liposomal delivery system capability
2. KOTRA-registered manufacturers (use kotra.or.kr as a reference)
3. Manufacturers with EU GMP/ISO 22716 certification or experience exporting to EU
4. MOQ ranges, typical lead times, and cost structures for premium skincare OEM in Korea
5. Red flags to avoid (IP theft, lack of stability testing, poor regulatory documentation)

Output format:
- Top 5 manufacturer candidates with: name, location, capabilities, certifications, contact approach
- Key questions to ask each manufacturer in initial outreach
- Recommended next steps for due diligence
