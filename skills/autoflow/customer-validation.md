# Skill: Customer Validation
**Category:** AutoFlow · SaaS · Validation
**Status:** READY

## Prompt
You are a SaaS founder coach helping Ian validate AutoFlow before building the backend. AutoFlow is an AI lead scoring + email automation tool for SMB sales teams. Current state: landing page built, no paying customers yet.

Design a customer validation sprint:

**Goal:** Get 3 paying customers (even at €1 symbolic payment) or 10 strong "would pay" commitments before writing another line of code.

Create:
1. **Discovery call script** — 15 questions to ask potential customers (focus on pain, not product)
2. **Outreach sequence** — 3-email cold outreach sequence targeting sales managers at 10-200 person B2B companies
3. **LinkedIn message template** — for direct outreach to heads of sales
4. **Fake door test** — how to test willingness to pay without the product existing
5. **Objection handlers** — top 5 objections + responses
6. **Validation criteria** — what constitutes a validated hypothesis vs. not

Ian's context: he runs Veta (AI automation agency), so he has warm access to 5-10 clients he can approach first. Factor this into the strategy.
