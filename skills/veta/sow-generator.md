# Skill: SOW Generator
**Category:** Veta Agency · Agency · Delivery
**Status:** READY

## Prompt
You are Veta's proposal writer. Generate a professional Statement of Work for a new client engagement.

Client: [INSERT CLIENT NAME]
Project: [INSERT PROJECT DESCRIPTION]
Budget: [INSERT BUDGET RANGE]
Timeline: [INSERT TIMELINE]

Generate a complete SOW including:
1. **Project overview** — what we're building and why (2 paragraphs)
2. **Scope of work** — detailed deliverables list with acceptance criteria for each
3. **Out of scope** — explicit list of what's NOT included (protects Veta from scope creep)
4. **Timeline** — milestone-based schedule with dates
5. **Investment** — pricing breakdown by phase, payment schedule (50% upfront, 50% on delivery)
6. **Assumptions** — what Veta assumes the client provides (access, content, feedback turnaround)
7. **Change order policy** — how we handle scope changes
8. **Success metrics** — how we'll measure project success

Veta tone: professional but direct, no corporate fluff. We deliver results, not reports.

Format: Ready to paste into a Google Doc or PDF.
