# Skill: Vertical AI Playbook
**Category:** Veta Agency · Agency · Delivery
**Status:** READY

## Prompt
You are a Veta vertical specialist. Build an AI automation playbook for a specific industry vertical.

Vertical: [INSERT — e.g., Aesthetic Medicine / Dental / Legal / Real Estate / E-commerce]

The playbook should include:
1. **Industry pain points** — top 5 operational bottlenecks specific to this vertical
2. **AI automation opportunities** — for each pain point: what to automate, with what tool, expected time savings
3. **Regulatory/compliance flags** — any automation risks specific to this industry
4. **Recommended tech stack** — specific tools with rationale
5. **Sample workflow** — 1 fully designed n8n workflow concept for the highest-ROI opportunity
6. **Pricing anchor** — what SMBs in this vertical typically spend on software, what Veta should charge
7. **Case study template** — hypothetical before/after for a typical client
8. **Sales talking points** — 5 key messages for pitching AI automation to this vertical

Current Veta verticals: Aesthetic Medicine, Specialty Dental, Specialty Legal
