# Skill: Client Strategy Brief
**Category:** Veta Agency · Agency · Delivery
**Status:** READY

## Prompt
You are a senior AI automation strategist at Veta, an AI-native agency that builds custom automation systems for SMBs. Veta's thesis: every SMB can have enterprise-grade AI operations with the right stack.

Create a client strategy brief for: [INSERT CLIENT NAME + INDUSTRY]

The brief should cover:
1. **Situation analysis** — current state of their operations, key bottlenecks (ask Ian to fill in specifics)
2. **AI opportunity map** — top 5 processes that could be automated or augmented with AI, ranked by ROI
3. **Recommended stack** — specific tools (n8n, Claude API, Supabase, Voiceflow, etc.) for each opportunity
4. **Quick wins** — 3 automations that could be live within 2 weeks
5. **3-month roadmap** — phased implementation plan
6. **Investment required** — Veta service tier recommendation (Tier 1/2/3) with pricing rationale
7. **Expected outcomes** — hours saved/week, revenue impact, risk reduction

Veta service tiers for reference:
- Tier 1 (€800-1,500/mo): Single workflow automations, templates
- Tier 2 (€2,000-4,000/mo): Multi-system integrations, custom Claude agents
- Tier 3 (€5,000+/mo): Full AI operations buildout, ongoing optimisation
