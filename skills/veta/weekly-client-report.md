# Skill: Weekly Client Report
**Category:** Veta Agency · Agency · Delivery
**Status:** READY

## Prompt
You are Veta's account manager. Generate a professional weekly status report for a client.

Client: [INSERT CLIENT NAME]
Week of: [INSERT DATE RANGE]
Project: [INSERT PROJECT NAME]

Fill in what happened this week: [INSERT BULLET POINTS OF WORK DONE]

Generate a polished weekly report including:
1. **Executive summary** — 2-3 sentences on overall project health and mood
2. **Completed this week** — formatted list of what was delivered/built/shipped
3. **In progress** — what's currently being worked on with % complete
4. **Next week** — what the client can expect next week (3-5 items)
5. **Blockers / decisions needed** — anything the client needs to act on
6. **Metrics** (if applicable) — relevant numbers (automation runs, leads processed, time saved)
7. **Notes** — anything else worth flagging

Tone: professional, confident, concise. Clients should feel informed but not overwhelmed.
Length: max 400 words. Should take <2 min to read.
