# Quickstart — your first company scan (≈20 minutes)

You've installed the skills (`INSTALL.md`) and skimmed `00-operating-manual.pdf`. Now run **Gate 0 (Scan)** on your own company — the fastest way to feel what the protocol does.

## 1. Open the protocol
In Claude Code, from `~/ai-native/`, open `PROTOCOL.md`. It's the spine — G0 → G4. Tell Claude Code: *"Follow PROTOCOL.md. Start Gate 0 (Scan) for my company."* It will load the infrastructure pillar (`pillars/2-infrastructure/`).

## 2. Feed it your reality
The scan asks what your company actually does — teams, tools, the repetitive computer work, where time leaks. This is **discovery**, and it's yours to answer (AI can't read your mind or your private systems). The RUNBOOK in `pillars/2-infrastructure/RUNBOOK.md` gives you the exact questions.

## 3. Get your as-is → to-be map
Output lands in a `.protocol/` folder: an **as-is** picture (how work happens now) and a **to-be** blueprint (what to automate, in what order). This is Gate 1.

## 4. The three things only you can do
As you move toward building (G2), the RUNBOOKs will pause for:
- **Access** — API keys / logins for the tools you want automated.
- **Discovery** — confirming what a process really does.
- **Trust** — approving anything before it goes live or acts on real data.

These are the human gates. The protocol makes them frictionless; it does not skip them. That's the honest deal.

## 5. Keep going, one gate at a time
G2 build (agent factory) → G3 secure → G4 investor-ready. Don't jump gates. Each PDF lesson in `COURSE.md` maps to the next gate.

**By day 90**, worked gate by gate, most of your company's repeatable computer work is running on agents and automations you built — secured, and legible to an investor.
