# AI-Native in 90 — Protocol Toolkit

**Become AI-native in 90 days.** This is the operating protocol for turning a normal company into one that runs most — or all — of its computer work through AI: scanning what you do, building the agents and automations to do it, securing them, and making the whole thing investor-ready.

You're holding the **self-serve edition**: the full methodology as a **course** plus the **Claude Code toolkit** that runs it. Same protocol AIS uses on paid engagements — packaged so you can run it yourself.

---

## What this is

A **5-gate protocol**, run in order:

| Gate | Pillar | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **G0 · Scan** | Infrastructure | AI scans your whole company — work, processes, tools, workflows — and maps as-is → to-be. |
| **G1 · Blueprint** | Infrastructure | The plan: which agents, automations, knowledge, and systems to build, and how the business should run on them. |
| **G2 · Build** | Agent factory | Build the agents & automations. Everything that *can* be automated with AI, gets automated. |
| **G3 · Secure** | Security | A gate, not an afterthought — nobody can reach your data or abuse your APIs. |
| **G4 · Investor-ready** | Investor | Infrastructure that makes an investor say "I need a stake in this." |

Security is a **gate** the build must pass, not a phase you skip.

## The honest part

This is **guided DIY, not a magic button.** AI does the hands-on building; *you* own the three things AI can't: **access** (keys, logins), **discovery** (what your business actually does), and **trust** (approving what goes live). The RUNBOOKs walk you through each of those so they're frictionless — but they're yours. Anyone promising "AI runs your whole company while you sleep, zero involvement" is selling you a fantasy. This sells you the real thing.
