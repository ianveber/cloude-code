# Biomasa — brand voice

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Tagline already in market (LinkedIn): **KO LES NI STRES**.

## Who speaks

A heating specialist who has been on roofs, in kotlovnice, and at fairs since 1998. Not a lifestyle influencer. Not a "greenwashing" NGO. Slovenian first. Du-form on Facebook/Instagram. Vi-form is acceptable on LinkedIn for municipal / investor posts.

## Audience split

| Channel | Who | Job of the post |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Homeowners, farmers, local community | Start a conversation, book a visit, remember Biomasa when the first cold week hits |
| Instagram | Same + visual proof (objects, fire, wood, team) | Save / share; feed the brand as real, not catalogue-only |
| LinkedIn | Investors, Petrol / municipal, installers, iPROM peers | Proof of delivery (kW, site, partner). Hire and B2B. |
| YouTube | People comparing kotli | Longer explanation; always point back to a product or ambassador |

## Voice rules

- Concrete numbers when they are on the website or a signed project: 5.000+ systems, 7–1000 kW, Eko sklad amounts **only** as currently stated on biomasa.si.
- Wood is comfort and competence, not poetry for its own sake. "Toplota iz srca" is a Fröling campaign line — use it when the visual is the S3v flame, not as filler.
- One offer per post. Do not mix kmetijski program and kotli in the same caption unless the event is a fair that shows both.
- CTA options (pick one): call / info@biomasa.si / biomasa.si / ambasador.biomasa.si / "napiši v komentar".
- Hashtags: Facebook 3–5. Instagram 8–12, mostly Slovenian + Fröling. LinkedIn 3 max.

## Claims we may use (sourced)

- Official Fröling representative for Slovenia.
- 5.000+ heating systems installed in SI + SE Europe (company site, 2026).
- BBC Nazarje: chips, technically dried chips, pellets, wood-gas CHP.
- Eko sklad: site currently advertises up to €5.000 / €4.000 nepovratna sredstva for a wood-biomass boiler — **re-check the live banner before every subsidy post**.
- AI Ambassador: iPROM + Retoba, public at ambasador.biomasa.si. David Špeh (technical director) is the named quote on the 13 Mar 2026 announcement.

## Off-brand

- "Revolutionary", "game-changer", emoji spam, fake urgency ("ONLY TODAY").
- Attacking heat pumps or gas as stupid. Contrast on running cost and local wood, not insults.
- Invented customer names or invented kW.
- English-first captions on Facebook.
- Posting the ambassador as if Veta built it.

## Visual direction

Warm wood, boiler rooms that look maintained (not filthy), fire/ember, Savinja valley, team in workwear, Fröling equipment with readable badges. Avoid stock "happy family on a couch" unless it is a real Biomasa household.
