How do I thin EPOLAC 2K primer for airless spray?
Thin only after both components are mixed. The data sheet gives this sequence for airless application:
  1. Mix component A with hardener B at 4 : 1 by weight. Stir mechanically for 3 minutes.
  2. Let the mix stand for 10 minutes (induction time) at 15 to 25 °C.
  3. Add EPOLAC Thinner 21, up to 10 % by volume, in small portions until the primer flows evenly off the stirrer.
  4. Airless setup: tip 0.017 to 0.021 in, 180 to 220 bar, 60 mesh filter.
  5. Pot life at 20 °C is 4 hours. Do not add thinner once the mix starts to thicken.
Source: TDS EPOLAC 2K Primer, section 4.2, rev. 03/2026
Thanks. And for brush application?
For brush or roller, thin with Thinner 21 up to 5 % by volume, or apply unthinned on small areas. Same mixing ratio and induction time.
Source: TDS EPOLAC 2K Primer, section 4.1
What is the recoat window at 10 °C?
At 10 °C the second coat can go on after 16 hours and within 5 days without abrading. After 5 days, sand lightly and remove the dust. Below 5 °C do not apply.
Source: TDS EPOLAC 2K Primer, section 5.1
Can it go over old alkyd paint?
No. EPOLAC 2K Primer is not compatible with alkyd coatings. Remove the old paint to bare steel (St 2 minimum), or ask sales for the alkyd-compatible system.
Source: TDS EPOLAC 2K Primer, section 3.4handed to sales
