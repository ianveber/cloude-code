# SPEED_PROTOCOLS — Spellman Performance + Tim Drenovc speed methodology

Two complementary speed development frameworks. Use when athlete needs sprint speed, acceleration, max velocity, or repeat-sprint ability (sprinters, soccer, basketball, rugby, hockey, court sports, MMA conditioning).

---

## A) FULL DYNAMIC WARM-UP (use before EVERY speed session)

Always 8-12 min. Non-negotiable before any sprint work.

### Drills (cca 20m each, walk back recovery)
- Jog (light, 200-400m)
- Backpedal
- Side Shuffle (with arms overhead)
- Forward Skip with Lunge & Reach
- Backward Skip with Lunge & Twist
- Backwards Reaching Walk
- Carioca
- 3 Step Straight Leg Kick
- Backwards Reaching Skip
- Side Shuffle-Lunge
- 3 Step Knee Pull (knee to chest)
- 3 Step Fold (Hamstring Scoops — split stance, fold over front leg)
- Backwards Reaching Run × 2

### Acceleration ramp (prep CNS for max effort)
- 75% Accel × 20m (×1)
- 85% Accel × 20m (×1)
- 95% Accel × 10m (+10m float-out) (×2)

Only after this ramp do you start the speed session proper.

---

## B) TOP SPEED PROTOCOL — Spellman Performance (6-week progression)

**Cilj:** maksimalna hitrost (max velocity). Sprint na full sprint speed po 20yd build-up. Razlika med "fly sprint" in običajnim sprintom je da športnik vstopi v merjeno cono že na top speedu (build-in iz 20yd).

**Format:** Fly sprint = 20yd build-in + measured zone. Vse na full sprint.

| Teden | Set 1 | Set 2 | Variation | Total fly volume |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10yd Fly × 3 (2 min rest) | 10yd Fly × 3 (2 min rest) | Float-Sprint-Float-Sprint × 2 (10-10-10-10yd) | 60yd |
| 2 | 15yd Fly × 3 (2 min rest) | 10yd Fly × 3 (2 min rest) | Slalom Swerve Sprint × 2 (30yd) | 75yd |
| 3 | 15yd Fly × 3 (2 min rest) | 15yd Fly × 3 (2 min rest) | Float-Sprint-Float-Sprint × 2 | 90yd |
| 4 | 20yd Fly × 3 (2 min rest) | 15yd Fly × 3 (2 min rest) | Float-Sprint-Float-Sprint × 2 | 105yd |
| 5 | 20yd Fly × 3 (2 min rest) | 20yd Fly × 3 (2 min rest) | Slalom Swerve Sprint × 2 (30yd) | 120yd |
| 6 | 20yd Fly × 3 (2 min rest) | — (peak/taper) | Slalom Swerve Sprint × 2 (30yd) | 60yd (taper) |

**Rest between sets:** 4 min. Rest between reps: 2 min. Full CNS recovery is mandatory — otherwise this becomes a conditioning session, not speed work.

**RPE:** vsaka fly sprint je 100% effort. Če RPE pade pod 95% → daljši odmor ali konec seansa.

## C) HILL WORK PROTOCOL — Spellman Performance (6-week progression)

**Cilj:** horizontal force, acceleration, posterior chain power, knee health.
Vse 20 hill sprint repeticije skozi vse tedne — razlika je v **organizaciji setov**. Volumen je konstanten (20), organizacija pa se shifta od več manjših setov proti večjim nepretrganim blokom.

| Teden | Sets × Reps | Rest med seti | Total čas |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 × 4 | 3 min | 27-32 min |
| 2 | 4 × 5 | 3 min | 24-29 min |
| 3 | 3 × (8, 8, 4) | 3 min | 21-26 min |
| 4 | 2 × 10 | 3 min | 18-23 min |
| 5 | 2 × 10 | 2 min (shorter rest) | 17-22 min |
| 6 | 1 × 20 | n/a | 15-20 min |

**Razdalja:** 30-40m hill, 6-8% naklon. Walk-back rest between reps (3-5 sekund).
**RPE:** vsaka repetition na 95-100% effort.

## D) TIM DRENOVC SLOVENIA RUGBY 7s — speed framework (3 tedni)

**Format:** 2 obvezna + 1 opcijski trening/teden. Vsi vsebujejo dinamično ogrevanje (sekcija A zgoraj).

### TEDEN 1 — UVAJALNI (RPE 6-7)
Cilj: tehnika sprint mehanike, nabira volumna, ne pretiravat.
- Več accelerations (10-30m), submaximal
- Drills heavy
- Volumen višji, intenziteta nižja

### TEDEN 2 — RAZVOJNI (RPE 7-8)
Volumen ostane, intenziteta narašča. Več max velocity dela.
- Fly sprints začnejo
- Manj submaximal volumen
- COD dela dodaš

### TEDEN 3 — VRHUNSKI (RPE 8-9, manjši volumen)
Manj ponovitev, najvišja intenziteta. Pred turnirjem deload.
- Vse na 100%
- Volumen –40% vs teden 2
- Polni odmori (4-5 min)
- 1-2 dni pred turnirjem: nič sprintov, le mobility + light jog

## E) KO UPORABIŠ KAJ

| Šport / situacija | Primarni protokol | Sekundarni |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint atletika (100m, 200m) | TOP SPEED (B) | Hill Work (C) za off-season |
| Soccer, rugby, AF | Tim Drenovc 3-tedenski (D) | TOP SPEED (B) v off-season |
| Basketball, court sports | TOP SPEED (B) + COD work | — |
| Combat sports (MMA, boxing) | Hill Work (C) za conditioning + acceleration | — |
| Off-season general athlete | Hill Work (C) | TOP SPEED v zadnjem 6-tedenskem bloku |
| In-season maintenance | Manj volumen — 1 speed session/teden, 60-70% volumen tedna 3 | — |

## PRAVILA SPEED DELA (always apply)

1. **Speed work je ALWAYS CNS-intensive.** Nikoli ne meči speed work na ujetrujen dan.
2. **Polni odmori.** Če športnik ne more reproducirati 95%+ effort → odmor ali končaj.
3. **Pred speed sessions: vsaj 24h od težkega leg dneva.** Ne speed po heavy squats.
4. **Speed sessions vedno PRED kondicijskim delom.** CNS-prvo, lactate-zadnje.
5. **Number of fly sprints na teden:** rekreativec max 4, klubska liga 6-8, polprofi 8-12, profi 12-16.
6. **Hill sprints na teden:** rekreativec 10-15, klubska liga 15-20, polprofi 20-25, profi 25-30.
7. **Always end with cool-down jog 5 min + static stretching** (hamstrings, hip flexors, calves).
