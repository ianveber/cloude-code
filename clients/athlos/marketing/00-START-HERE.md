## THE REALISTIC PLAN — the 20% that actually moves the needle
The five detail files are the full system. But you are one person also building the app, so **build on the floor, treat everything above it as upside.** In priority order (from the red-team, `06-iman-redteam.md §4`):

1. **Wake the warm list + mine your personal network by hand.** *(This IS the launch.)* Re-engagement emails to the Beehiiv list + personally DM every athlete, coach, and training partner you know, offering the free 7-day Tim plan. A real spreadsheet: Hot / Warm / Cold. If you did *only this*, you'd still get a launch.
2. **Build the lead magnet** — Tim's 7-day sport-specific PDF + a quick assessment (the Coach brain generates the base). It turns "check out my app" into "here's a free plan for your sport." Cheap, high-leverage.
3. **Plug every leak in the backend** — Beehiiv confirm-nudge (unconfirmed opt-ins = found money), Stripe checkout + abandonment DM, failed-payment dunning. Mostly one-time setup; at 40–150 leads every leak is a countable buyer.
4. **One rough VSL + a 5-email launch sequence.** Not a polished production — one honest 8-min founder-to-camera video ("why generic training fails → the system → the app working → the offer") that every warm lead receives, plus cart-open → objection → scarcity → final-call emails. Ship it rough, re-cut after launch.
5. **Light daily documentation on the PERSONAL account only + reply to every DM.** One account, "dan X/30" raw phone documentation, one Content Day/week that makes 3 shorts *if* it makes anything. The engagement *is* the funnel work. Documentation over production.

### Cut or defer to month 2+ (so the app still ships)
- ❌ The full second (@athlos) content operation → make it a **link-and-counter repository, ~2×/week** (Tim clip + the "X/100" counter). Don't run two content machines.
- ❌ The 10-shorts-from-one-video remix engine → post the clips that are genuinely good, ignore the math (nobody's watching enough yet).
- ❌ The Hellenic/mythology *content* pillar → keep the visual look (free differentiation), cut the mythology posts for 30 days.
- ❌ The free live launch session as a mandatory tentpole → high-stress, high-fail for a first-timer; a launch-day Story series + personal DMs does the same job. Keep it *only* if Weeks 1–3 went smoothly.
- ❌ Weekly YouTube long-form → one video (the VSL) is enough for launch.

**One-sentence version:** *Wake the list, hand them a free Tim plan, plug every leak, send one honest video + five emails, and DM every human by hand. Everything else is month 2.*

## ⚠️ Two things to lock BEFORE Day 1 (biggest risks)
1. **Lock Tim's minimum content commitment in writing (minutes/week).** ~40% of the content strategy and 100% of the authority strategy structurally depend on him appearing. Terms are still open (equity/rev-share/retainer). If he can't commit, build the fallback where you carry the science yourself, co-signed *"kar sem se naučil od Tima."* An unsigned dependency under the whole plan is the #2 launch-killer.
2. **Verify your personal network is actually right-fit athletes.** The whole list-growth math (lever #1 = 40–80 leads) assumes you can personally DM 40–80 Slovenian nogomet/košarka athletes. If your graph is mostly tech/general friends, that lever shrinks and the targets halve (→ 30–60 leads, 2–6 buyers). Check this honestly first.
