## 0. The core reframe (read this first)

Iman's #1 rule: **you are not a content creator, you are a business owner who uses content to drive a business** (Intentional Brand). Before any post exists, the click-path to a signup must exist. That means the two accounts are not "two feeds." They are two organs of one funnel:

- **@ianveber (personal)** = the **trust engine / traffic source**. Reach, face, story, opinions, "THE JOURNEY." This is where humans decide *"I trust this guy."*
- **@athlos (app account)** = the **conversion surface / product proof**. Product, science, results, waitlist. This is where trust gets converted into a waitlist signup and later a €29–89 subscription.

Personal DRIVES. App CONVERTS. If you only remember one sentence from this doc, it's that.

**Blind-spot check up front:** Iman runs 30+ feeder accounts pushing 250–300M views/month into one hub. Ian has ~a dozen waitlist subs and effectively zero followers. So we do NOT copy his *structure* (a fleet of siloed accounts) — at Ian's scale that just splits already-thin attention across empty rooms. We copy his *mechanics* (map content→customer, optimize for leads not views, one no-leak backend). Two accounts is the correct number for a cold start. Not one (personal + product blur kills conversion trust), not five (dilution). Two.

## 1. What each account is FOR + the interlock mechanics

### 1a. Division of labor

| | **@ianveber (personal)** | **@athlos (app account)** |
|---|---|---|
| Job (Iman term) | Growth + nurture, "makes you known/liked" | Conversion + social proof, "makes you money" |
| Metric | Reach → profile clicks → *follows on @athlos* | Waitlist signups, then subs |
| Voice | 1st person, raw, opinionated, "gradim v živo" | Brand voice — confident, mythic, evidence-led |
| Content | Build-in-public, Ian's own training, hot takes, story | Product demos, Tim science, beta results, waitlist momentum |
| CTA | "Sledi @athlos / na čakalni listi je povezava" | "Rezerviraj mesto — link v biu" |

**Iman mapping:** This is his *Content Siloing* (Rule 5) done honestly for a cold start. He silos to protect lead quality at scale; Ian silos to protect *trust*. A personal account that hard-sells a product looks desperate; a product account that's all founder-face looks like it has no product. Splitting the jobs lets each do its job cleanly.

### 1b. The hand-off moves (this is the whole machine)

The single failure mode Iman names over and over is the **leak**: attention arrives and quietly leaves without becoming a lead (No-Leak Backend). Every hand-off below exists to plug one leak. Ian must build these as literal, repeatable moves — not "sometimes mention the app."

**HAND-OFF 1 — Personal Reel → @athlos follow → waitlist (the daily loop)**
Every personal Reel ends with a **fixed spoken + on-screen outro** (this is Iman's *Signature Style*, Rule 10 — an ownable, repeated close that trains the audience what to do):
> *"Vse skupaj gradim v aplikaciji ATHLOS. Če hočeš biti med prvimi 100 — link je na @athlos."*
Pinned comment (auto, every post): `Prvih 100 članov zaklene ceno za vedno → @athlos → link v biu → čakalna lista.`
So the path is: **personal Reel (reach) → verbal+comment CTA → profile @athlos → link-in-bio → waitlist form.** Four steps, zero leaks.

**HAND-OFF 2 — The "collab post" bridge**
On the 1–2 biggest posts per week, use IG **Collab** (co-author) between @ianveber and @athlos. The post lands on *both* grids and both audiences see it. This is the cheapest possible way to seed @athlos followers from Ian's (larger) personal reach at a cold start — one tap, no re-upload. Iman would call this borrowing distribution; at Ian's scale it's the only free distribution he has, so use it aggressively (not once a month — 1–2×/week).

**HAND-OFF 3 — Story chain (daily, high-intent)**
Personal Story (Ian's face, casual) → swipe-up/sticker "Poll: A ali B" to warm them → next Story frame = **@athlos mention sticker** → next frame = **link sticker to waitlist**. Iman: *"Email nurtures interest, WhatsApp/DM controls behavior"* — Stories are Ian's behavior-control channel because they're where the parasocial trust is highest. Put the waitlist link in a Story ~every 2nd day, never naked — always after a value/entertainment frame.

**HAND-OFF 4 — YouTube deep video → description → waitlist (the converting layer)**
YouTube long-form is where the *decision* gets made (Iman: the deep video is "core converting"). Every YouTube video description, top line, pinned comment:
> *"👉 ATHLOS čakalna lista (prvih 100 zaklene ceno): [link]"*
End screen + a mid-roll verbal callout at the value peak, not just the outro.

**HAND-OFF 5 — @athlos → @ianveber (the reverse tap, for trust)**
The app account periodically points *back* at the founder: *"Za aplikacijo stoji Ian → poglej kako jo gradi v živo: @ianveber."* This closes the loop so someone who lands on the product account cold can go verify there's a real human behind it. Trust flows both directions; money flows one direction (into the waitlist).

### 1c. Multi-Funnel Lead Capture (adapted)

Iman captures leads across many funnels. Ian has essentially **one destination (the Beehiiv waitlist) reachable via multiple on-ramps.** Don't build multiple funnels — build multiple *doors to the same funnel*:

- Door 1: link-in-bio (both accounts) → waitlist
- Door 2: pinned comment on every post → waitlist
- Door 3: Story link sticker → waitlist
- Door 4: DM keyword auto-reply (see §7) → waitlist
- Door 5: YouTube description + pinned comment → waitlist

**Blind-spot check:** Iman would run a $75 tripwire → $2,000 workshop escalation. Ian has no product live and a 30-day runway — a paid tripwire now would burn the warm list. The **waitlist IS the front-end offer** for these 30 days. The only "purchase" pre-launch is the emotional one: claiming a spot in the first 100. Keep it single-destination. Escalation to €29–89 tiers happens *at* launch, not before.

## 4. Social-proof engine — manufacturing credibility from ~zero

This is the hardest cold-start problem and where Iman's advice most needs adjusting. **Iman assumes existing social proof (millions of followers = automatic authority).** Ian has none. So we can't *borrow* proof — we have to *build* it from primitives. Four primitives, in priority order:

### 4a. Borrowed authority — Tim Drenovc (start here, works day 1)
Ian has zero follower-proof but he has a **credentialed expert** attached. That is transferable authority Iman would kill for at a cold start. Front-load Tim hard in the first 2 weeks:
- **"Tim razloži"** micro-series: 30–60s clips of Tim explaining ONE real concept (periodizacija, RPE, zakaj počitek ni lenoba). Iman's *Documentation before Education* + *One Step Ahead* — Tim doesn't need to be famous, only correct and one step ahead of the viewer.
- Caption formula: hook (a myth) → Tim busts it → "točno to logiko ima vgrajeno ATHLOS."
- Example post: *"Zakaj bolj ko treniraš, počasnejši postajaš? Fiziolog Tim Drenovc razloži napako, ki jo dela 90 % rekreativcev. 🧵"*

### 4b. Founder-in-the-arena — Ian as beta subject #1 (works day 1, no users needed)
No beta testers yet? **Ian is the first beta tester.** Document his own training run on ATHLOS's plan. This is Iman's *Documentation before Education* — you don't need a client testimonial when you can show the founder eating his own cooking, on camera, with numbers.
- Weekly: *"Teden 2 na ATHLOS planu. Kaj se je spremenilo v mojih številkah 👇"* — show real logged data (RPE, kg, sprint time). Real numbers, even unimpressive ones, out-convert stock-photo hype.
- This also seeds Pillar 3 (Dokaz) before any external result exists.

### 4c. Beta results — engineered scarcity of proof (weeks 2–4)
Recruit 5–10 beta testers *from the warm waitlist* (DM them — see §7). Even 3 real people generate:
- **Before/prvi vtis** cards: *"Prvih 7 dni z ATHLOS — kaj pravi [Ime], rokoborec, 24 let."* Screenshot a real DM reaction. Iman's whole social-proof machine is just *documented real reactions at scale* — Ian starts at n=3, and that's honest and enough to break the "nobody uses this" objection.
- Micro-testimonial Reels: tester reads their own 2-sentence reaction to camera. Raw phone video > polished — it reads as real.
- **Blind-spot check:** do NOT fake results or invent testers. A small Slovenian market is a *village* — one fabricated testimonial and Ian is done. Scarcity of proof is fine ("smo v zaprti beti z 8 športniki"); fake proof is fatal. Frame the smallness as exclusivity, not weakness.

### 4d. Waitlist-count momentum (the compounding proof — works day 1)
This is the single best cold-start proof mechanic because it needs no users, just a number that moves. Iman uses live challenge counters / prize pools as engagement drivers; Ian's version is the **"prvih 100 mest" counter.**
- Post the count as it moves: *"Še 41 mest do polovice. Ko doseže 100, se zgodnja cena zapre za vedno."*
- Milestone posts at 25/50/75/90/100 — each is a fresh reason to post AND a scarcity trigger.
- Screenshot the counter into Stories every few days. Motion = proof of demand even with tiny absolute numbers. 60/100 *feels* like momentum; "we have 60 signups" feels small. Frame as the fraction.

**The credibility ladder (how proof compounds over 30 days):**
`Tim (borrowed authority) → Ian as beta #1 (founder proof) → 3–8 beta testers (peer proof) → waitlist counter climbing (demand proof) → launch-day "prvih 100 skoraj polnih" (urgency proof)`. Each rung makes the next post more believable. You are not waiting for credibility — you are manufacturing it in sequence.

## 6. The mythic-athlete (Hellenic) angle — how to use it without being cringe

The Zeus/oracle/marble aesthetic is a **memorability and identity layer**, not the substance. Iman's *Signature Style* (Rule 10): an ownable motif that compounds recognition. The Hellenic theme is Ian's motif. The rule that keeps it from being cringe:

> **The myth is the frame; the benefit is the payload. Never let the myth be the whole point of a post.**

Concretely — the safe/cringe line:

- ✅ **Aesthetic as wrapper:** marble/gold color grade, Cinzel/Marcellus type on title cards, a subtle Olympus/oracle motif in the logo sting and Story frames. Visual identity people recognize in the feed. This is 100% safe.
- ✅ **Myth as analogy that pays off in a real point:** *"Bogovi so gledali z Olimpa — ATHLOS gleda tvoje številke"* works because it lands on a concrete benefit (data-driven training). The myth earned its place.
- ✅ **"Oracle" as a content format:** a recurring *"Prerokba tedna"* where the oracle just delivers one real Tim training rule. The costume is fun; the content is legit science.
- ❌ **Myth with no payload:** posting a Zeus statue with "UNLEASH YOUR INNER GOD 🔱⚡" and nothing else. That's the cringe zone — costume without substance. Ban it.
- ❌ **Overdoing the voice:** don't write captions in fake-archaic Slovenian. Ian talks like a normal young Slovenian founder; the *visuals* carry the myth, the *words* stay human and modern. Mismatch (mythic words + modern product) is what reads as try-hard.

**Cold-start advantage:** the Hellenic look is genuinely differentiating in a Slovenian fitness feed full of neon-gym content. Iman says packaging is 80% — the marble/gold identity gives Ian a scroll-stopping *look* before he has follower-proof. It's the cheapest competitive moat he has right now. Lean on the aesthetic hard; keep the mythology as seasoning.

## 7. Community & DM system — Iman's DM-qualification move, adapted

Iman: *"Email nurtures interest, DM/WhatsApp controls behavior."* Every new follower, comment, and reaction is a lead that should be walked toward the waitlist by hand while the numbers are small enough to do it by hand. **At a cold start this is Ian's superpower, not a chore** — Iman can't personally DM his audience; Ian *can*, and personal DMs from the founder convert absurdly well at n<500 followers. Do the unscalable thing on purpose.

### 7a. The rule
Nobody engages without getting pulled one step toward the waitlist. Not aggressively — one warm, human step.

### 7b. Comment → DM handoff (public, then private)
When someone comments anything positive on an @athlos or @ianveber post:
- Public reply (builds proof for lurkers): *"Hvala 🙏 si športnik ali treniraš resno? Ti pošljem nekaj v DM."*
- Then DM (see scripts).

### 7c. DM scripts (Slovenian, copy-paste)

**New follower / warm commenter — open + qualify (Iman's discovery move, softened):**
```
Živjo! Hvala, ker spremljaš ATHLOS 🏛️
Kratko vprašanje, da vem, kaj ti pošiljati: kateri šport treniraš in kako resno (rekreativno / tekmovalno / profi)?
```
> *Why:* this is Iman's sales-call *discovery* phase compressed into one line. The answer both qualifies the lead AND tells Ian which content to serve.

**If they answer (they're a real athlete) — the waitlist push:**
```
Super — točno za takšne gradim ATHLOS. 🙌
Odpiramo samo prvih 100 mest po zgodnji ceni (zaklene se za vedno). Ti pošljem link, da si rezerviraš svojega?
```
Wait for a "ja" (micro-commitment — Iman's commitment escalation), THEN drop the link. The yes before the link is what makes it land as help, not spam.

**If they say yes:**
```
Evo 👇 [waitlist link]
Ko boš notri, mi napiši svoj šport — dam te na seznam za beta test, če te zanima. 💪
```
> *Why:* the beta-test offer is Iman's *disqualification-as-value* — being "chosen" for beta raises perceived value and simultaneously fills Ian's social-proof engine (§4c). Same DM, two jobs.

**Cold-follower who never engages — re-engagement (send once, no spam):**
```
Živjo! Vidim, da spremljaš — pošteno vprašanje: kaj te je pritegnilo k ATHLOS, znanost za treningom ali sama aplikacija? Rad slišim, gradim to za ljudi kot si ti. 🙏
```

**Beta-tester recruit (DM to warm waitlist subs):**
```
Živjo [Ime]! Si na čakalni listi za ATHLOS — hvala 🙏
Odpiramo zaprto beto za 8 športnikov PRED lansiranjem. Zastonj, v zameno za iskren feedback. Te zanima? Rabim samo tvoj šport + koliko treniraš.
```

### 7d. The daily DM discipline (the load-bearing habit)
Iman spends 70–80% of agency time on outreach. Ian's cold-start equivalent: **15 min/day of DMs** — reply to every comment, open a DM with every new relevant follower, walk warm ones to the waitlist. This is the highest-ROI 15 minutes in the whole plan because at n<500 followers a single founder DM converts better than any post. When it stops scaling (post-launch, hundreds of DMs/day), that's when you templatize with keyword auto-replies (Door 4). Not before.

**Keyword auto-reply (set up now, cheap):** comment `MESTO` on any post → auto-DM: *"Evo tvoj link za prvih 100 mest 👇 [waitlist]. Napiši mi svoj šport, ko si notri! 🏛️"* — turns every "how do I join?" comment into a captured lead with zero manual work. This is Ian's one legitimate piece of automation-at-cold-start.

## 9. What to steal from Iman vs what to ignore (cold-start summary)

**STEAL (works at any scale):**
- Intentional Brand — map every post to the waitlist before making it.
- Optimize for leads (waitlist signups), not views. UTM every door.
- No-Leak Backend — every touchpoint funnels to one destination.
- Signature Style — fixed outro CTA + Hellenic motif for compounding recognition.
- Documentation before Education — Ian-as-beta + Tim clips = proof from zero.
- DM-qualification — the unscalable founder DM is Ian's edge, not his handicap.

**IGNORE / ADJUST (assumes scale Ian doesn't have):**
- 30+ feeder accounts → **two accounts only.** Splitting thin attention = death.
- Deliberately-small high-ticket channel → Ian's tiers are €29–89, not $5K; he needs *some* reach, so personal account chases reach while app account stays tight. Don't cap reach on purpose yet.
- $75 tripwire → $2K workshop escalation → **no paid front-end pre-launch.** The waitlist is the offer for 30 days.
- Paid clipper army → **free Collab posts + organic Reels.** No budget, no clippers.
- Value-stack anchoring against crossed-out $ figures → save for the launch page, not the pre-launch feed.
