# ATHLOS — 30-Day Launch Plan (day-by-day, both accounts)

**Strategy in English. Every content asset in Slovenian.**
Applying Iman Gadzhi's launch playbook — documentary build-up → content chain → warm-list open-cart — **adapted for a cold start**: near-zero audience, solo founder, small Slovenian market, 100% organic, 30 days to MVP live.

---

## How to read this plan

Two accounts run in parallel from Day 1:

- **@ian.veber (personal)** — the *trust engine*. Documentation-before-education (Iman on-ramp #10). Ian's face, Ian's journey building ATHLOS + training with Tim. This is where reach and parasocial trust get built. IG daily + YouTube weekly.
- **@athlos.si / @athlos (app account)** — the *conversion surface*. Product, Tim's science, athlete results, the offer. Lower volume, higher intent. This is the "deliberately small channel" (Iman #3) — it does not need to go viral; it needs to convert the warm list.

**The interlock (locked decision):** personal *drives*, app *converts*. Every personal post that lands mentions "gradim ATHLOS" and points curious people at @athlos. The app account never has to cold-start reach — it inherits Ian's warm traffic.

### The Iman frameworks you'll see named below
- **(Intentional Brand)** — map content → buyer before making it
- **(Leads-not-views)** — judge by qualified leads, not impressions
- **(Small Channel)** — narrow buyer-only channel, converts at low view counts
- **(3:1 Growth-to-Nurture)** — ~75% reach, ~25% relationship/conversion
- **(Flywheel VSL)** — one evergreen sales video everything links to
- **(Documentation-first)** — document the build, don't teach polished tutorials
- **(10-Video Challenge)** — reps before exposure to kill camera fear
- **(Problem Farming)** — turn buyer pain into an endless content backlog
- **(Content Rehashing)** — repackage old pieces, change only hook/thumbnail
- **(Disqualification Positioning)** — state who it's NOT for to raise value
- **(No-Leak Backend)** — every touchpoint engineered so no lead leaks out
- **(Documentary Build-up)** — public countdown/launch-event scarcity

### The permanent blind-spot correction (applies to all 4 weeks)
Iman's playbook assumes millions of monthly views, a 30-person team, and years of runway. **Ian has none of that.** So across this whole plan:
- "Go viral" is replaced by **"be findable and convert the warm ~dozen + their referrals."** The list is the asset, not the algorithm.
- The VSL is built **small and fast** (one good take), not a Netflix-grade production.
- **Realistic solo-founder time budget: ~1.5–2 hrs/day on content max**, because Ian is also *building the app that has to ship in 30 days*. Every day below is scoped to that. If a day is heavy on build, content is a repurpose, not a new shoot.
- **Batch, don't drip-create.** One "Content Day" per week produces most of the week's short-form (Iman #10). The daily table shows what *publishes*, not what's filmed that morning.

## Day 31+ — Right after launch (keep the flywheel spinning)

The moment cart closes, the game switches from *acquisition* to *retention + compounding* — because for a subscription, the profit lives in month 2+, not the first sale (Iman's No-Leak/retention-engineering point). **Week 5 = obsessive white-glove onboarding of every founding athlete** (handwritten-note energy, fast support, make sure each one completes their first real workout and feels the system "know" them — this crushes early churn and manufactures your first testimonials). Meanwhile the content engine does *not* stop: the VSL stays pinned and every new post keeps linking back to it (Flywheel), Ian shifts from "launch build" to "**results build-in-public**" — documenting real founding-athlete progress as the next reach + proof loop — and the whole 30-day archive gets **rehashed** (new hooks/covers, Iman #8) to keep reach alive at near-zero effort while Ian returns focus to the product. The waitlist reopens as an evergreen "**naslednji val**" (next cohort) list so late-comers keep flowing in behind the closed early-bird, and the first testimonials feed straight into the app account's authority. Cadence drops to a sustainable **2–3 posts/week + 1 YouTube/week + 1 weekly email** — the compounding, not-burning-out rhythm a solo founder can actually hold for the long game, with the *next* early-bird tier (or the B2B club motion) as the following campaign once the founding cohort is retained and referring.
