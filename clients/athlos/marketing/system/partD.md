# 4 · Distribution & Social Presence

> This chapter is the operating manual for where ATHLOS shows up in public and how attention gets routed from a stranger's feed into a confirmed waitlist lead. Everything here inherits from `01-brand-foundation.md` (dual-brand architecture) and `04-funnel-sales-system.md` (the no-leak funnel). If anything here contradicts those, they win.
>
> **The one rule that governs this whole chapter:** attention flows **Ian → @athlos → waitlist**, one direction, never backward. Ian's face and story pull strangers in; he *points* them at the app account and the free plan. The app account never has to be charismatic — it only has to be credible and easy to opt into. We do not run two content machines. We run **one founder, one camera, one funnel**, cross-posted intelligently.
>
> **Cold-start honesty (the blind-spot check runs through every section):** we have a tiny starting audience, a solo-ish team also shipping the app, €0 ad budget, and a ~2.1M-person Slovenian market where the serious-athlete niche is tens of thousands, not millions. Iman's distribution playbook assumes 30+ feeder accounts and a paid clipper army. **We have Ian and one Content Day a week.** So wherever Iman says "scale the machine," we read it as "pick the two moves that a founder can actually sustain, and do those relentlessly." Volume targets in this chapter have a **floor** (what must happen even on a bad week) and a **stretch** (what a good week looks like). Build to the floor; treat the stretch as upside.

## 4.1 Platform roles — who does what, and the interlock

Every platform and every account has exactly ONE job. This is Iman's **Content Siloing** applied honestly at cold-start scale: we don't split effort evenly across a dozen surfaces, we assign each surface a single purpose so nothing is diluted and Ian always knows *why* he's posting a given thing.

### The account map

| Surface | Account | Single job | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram (Ian)** | personal — **the priority, ~80% of effort** | Trust engine. Daily founder presence, THE JOURNEY, the face people follow. | First person, human, opinionated — "jaz" |
| **Instagram (@athlos)** | app — **lean, ~20%** | Proof-and-offer repository. Tim's science, product demos, the counter, testimonials, the offer. The place Ian *sends* people. | Brand/product — "mi" + Tim's authority |
| **YouTube long-form (Ian)** | personal | The deep converter. The one "core video" (VSL) + occasional long build-in-public. Lives under Ian because the buyer must trust the founder-athlete before the app. | Ian, unhurried, in-depth |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Ian's channel | Reach + a searchable back-catalog that compounds. Same clips as Reels. | Same as Reels |
| **TikTok (optional)** | Ian | Pure cross-post overflow. Zero extra production. Post the same vertical clip if it takes 30 seconds; skip it on a busy week without guilt. | Same as Reels |

**Why Instagram + YouTube are primary and TikTok is optional:** IG is where the Slovenian 16–26 athlete audience actually lives and where a person (not a logo) gets followed. YouTube is where the *converting* long-form lives and where **search** works in our favor for years (a Short titled "hitrejši v nogometu" keeps getting found long after we post it). TikTok's Slovenian discovery is real but thinner for this niche and the algorithm won't reward a cold account fast enough to justify dedicated production in 30 days — so it's a free cross-post, never a third machine.

### The personal ↔ app interlock (the hand-off moves)

This is the load-bearing part of the whole chapter. Attention that lands on Ian is worthless unless it moves to the app account and then to the waitlist. These are the **exact mechanical hand-offs** that move it. Use them deliberately — every one of them is a place a lead leaks if it's missing.

1. **The spoken outro (Ian → waitlist).** Every long-form and most Reels end with Ian saying it out loud, not just captioning it. Signature line, used verbatim every time so it compounds recognition (Iman's **signature style** motif):
   > *"Gradim ATHLOS — link je v biografiji. Prvih 100 zaklene ceno za vedno."*
   Spoken > written: people skip captions, they don't skip the last spoken line if the video earned the watch.

2. **The pinned comment (every post, both accounts).** The first comment is pinned by the poster and is *always* the same click-path — the free-plan opt-in, never a bare "check my app." On Ian's posts it reads as a gift; on @athlos it reads as the offer. See the first-comment formula in §4.3.

3. **Instagram Collab posts (Ian ⨯ @athlos).** When Ian posts a piece that features the product or Tim, publish it as an **IG Collab** so it appears natively on *both* grids and both audiences' feeds from one upload. This is the single cheapest way to seed the @athlos account without running it as a separate machine — Ian's reach carries the app account's early growth.

4. **Story chains (Ian → @athlos → link).** Ian's Story is the daily bridge. A chain runs: Story 1 = the human moment/hook → Story 2 = "poglej, kaj sem danes zgradil" pointing at @athlos → Story 3 = the **link sticker** to the opt-in. Ian **@-mentions @athlos** in the chain so a tap moves the viewer onto the app account's profile. Re-share @athlos's proof/testimonial posts to Ian's Story so the app account borrows Ian's audience.

5. **Link-in-bio (the one destination).** Both bios point to the **same** opt-in page (the free 7-day Tim plan → Beehiiv). Not a link tree with ten options — **one link, one decision.** Ian's bio: soft, "delam na tem." @athlos bio: hard, "prvih 100." Both buttons, same page. Every extra link on that page is a leak.

**The interlock in one line:** *Ian earns the attention → the spoken outro + Story chain + @-mention move it to @athlos → the pinned comment + link-in-bio move it to the waitlist.* If a piece of content has none of these hand-offs, it's a pure nurture piece for the core (fine — but know it, per Iman's **Intentional Brand**: map the click-path before you film, or don't film it).

## 4.2 Posting cadence — a realistic founder-led schedule

Honesty first: **one person also building an app cannot sustain a 30-account content operation.** The cadence below is what a solo founder with one Content Day a week can actually hold. Everything is expressed as **floor / stretch**. Hit the floor on a bad week and you're fine. The floor is designed so the funnel never goes dark, not so the algorithm is maximally fed.

### Weekly cadence per surface

| Surface | Account | **Floor** (bad week) | **Stretch** (good week) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IG Reels | Ian | 3 / week | 5 / week | Content Day clips |
| IG Stories | Ian | daily, 1 chain | daily, 2–3 chains | Phone, raw, THE JOURNEY |
| IG feed (non-Reel) | Ian | 1 / week | 2 / week | Carousel or photo |
| IG Reels | @athlos | 2 / week | 3 / week | Same clips, Collab where product/Tim |
| IG Stories | @athlos | 2 / week | daily re-shares | Re-share Ian + counter updates |
| YT long-form | Ian | **0** most weeks | 1 (the VSL, once) | Content Day |
| YT Shorts | Ian | 3 / week | 5 / week | **Same clips as Reels** |
| TikTok | Ian | 0 (optional) | 3 / week | Cross-post overflow |
| Beehiiv email | — | **1 / week minimum** | 2 / week in launch window | See `04` §4 |

**The non-negotiable floor** (if literally nothing else happens in a week):
- **Daily Ian Story** (the funnel's heartbeat — this *is* the DM/engagement surface).
- **3 Reels + 3 Shorts** from one Content Day (same clips, two platforms).
- **1 Beehiiv email to the warm list.** The list is the launch; it must never go a week dark.

**Read the cadence honestly against Iman:** Iman posts to 30+ feeders daily because he has a team. We are running **one Content Day per week** that produces the raw material for everything above, plus daily phone Stories that cost minutes. If Ian ever feels the schedule pulling him off building the app, the correct move is to drop to the floor — never to skip the email or the Stories, which are where the money is; drop the *reach* posts (extra Reels, TikTok), never the *nurture* channel.

### The weekly rhythm (what a normal week looks like)

- **Mon — Content Day.** Batch-film. One long-ish talk-to-camera (or the VSL when it's VSL week) + 3–5 short setups. Good audio + clean background beats fancy gear (Iman's rule). This one day feeds the whole week.
- **Tue–Fri — post + engage.** Reels/Shorts drop from Monday's batch; daily Stories from the phone; **the daily engagement ritual (§4.5) runs every day regardless of whether anything was posted.**
- **One evening — write & send the Beehiiv email.** Founder voice, from Ian.
- **Sat/Sun — light or off.** A Story or two. Rest is allowed; the machine survives a quiet weekend.

## 4.3 The short-form engine — one Content Day → ~8–10 clips everywhere

This is the production heart. Iman's principle: **you are building assets, not running on a treadmill.** One filming session becomes a week of distribution. Here is the exact flow.

### From one Content Day to the feed

1. **Film once (Monday).** In one session Ian shoots one longer talk-to-camera piece (5–12 min — the "core" idea of the week) plus 3–5 standalone short setups (a hook + one point each). Raw phone footage is fine; the standard is *clarity and energy*, not production polish.
2. **Cut to ~8–10 vertical clips.** From the long piece, pull the 3–5 strongest self-contained moments (a myth busted, a number, a before/after, a hot take). Add the 3–5 standalone shorts. That's **~8–10 clips from one day.**
3. **Route the clips** (this is the honest cold-start version of Iman's remix engine — post the clips that are *genuinely good*, don't force the full 10× math when nobody's watching enough yet):
   - **Best 3–5 → Ian's IG Reels** across the week.
   - **Same clips → Ian's YouTube Shorts** (identical file, but a search-optimized title — see §4.4).
   - **1–2 product/Tim clips → @athlos as IG Collab** (appears on both grids from one upload).
   - **Overflow → TikTok** if it's a 30-second cross-post, skip otherwise.
   - **The long piece → YouTube long-form** only on VSL weeks or when it's genuinely a standalone build-in-public episode; otherwise it's just clip fuel.
4. **Every clip carries the three hand-off elements:** on-screen hook (first frame), spoken outro, pinned first-comment CTA.

**Do NOT re-edit per platform.** Same vertical clip, same subtitles, different *title/caption* per platform. Re-editing is the treadmill; re-titling is the asset move. (Post-launch, when a back-catalog exists, this is exactly where Iman's **Content Rehashing** kicks in — new title/thumbnail on an old clip to unlock latent reach at ~zero cost. Note it now; it becomes the #1 anti-burnout lever in month 2, not week 1.)

### The caption formula (Slovenian) — used on every clip

A caption is not a description, it's a **second hook + a reason to act.** Structure, top to bottom:

```
[HOOK LINE — one punchy sentence that restates the video's promise]

[1–3 SHORT VALUE LINES — the takeaway, so even a non-watcher learns something]

[CTA LINE — always the same click-path, pointing at the pinned comment/bio]

[3–5 niche hashtags — see §4.4]
```

**Drop-in example (a speed/explosiveness clip):**

> Tvoj "klasičen split" iz Instagrama te dela POČASNEJŠEGA. 🧵
>
> Nogomet in košarka ne potrebujeta bicepsov za pozo — potrebujeta eksplozivnost, hitrost spremembe smeri in moč, ki se prenese na igrišče. Generični program tega ne da.
>
> Napisal sem ti brezplačen 7-dnevni Timov načrt za tvoj šport → **link v prvem komentarju.** Prvih 100 zaklene ceno za vedno.
>
> #nogomet #košarka #hitrost #eksplozivnost #športnitrening

### On-screen hooks (the first frame)

The first frame decides watch-through, and watch-through decides reach (Iman: **packaging is 80%**). Rules:
- **Text on the very first frame**, big and readable on a phone, high contrast, safe from the UI overlay.
- It states a **specific, sport-named** promise or provokes, e.g. *"Zakaj si počasnejši, čeprav treniraš več"*, *"To dela vsak slovenski nogometaš narobe"*, *"Postal sem hitrejši v 3 tednih — tukaj je kako"*.
- Name the **enemy** where possible (generic bodybuilding splits) — it's the sharpest hook we have and it's ownable.
- **Never** a vague hook ("moji nasveti za trening"). Specific + sport-named beats clever every time in a small market.

### The first-comment CTA (pinned, every post)

Pin the first comment yourself the moment you post. It's the click-path that IG's link-suppression on captions can't kill. Formula:

> 👇 Brezplačen 7-dnevni načrt za tvoj šport (Tim Drenovc) — link v biografiji. Napiši mi, kateri šport treniraš, in ti povem, kje začeti.

That last line — *"napiši mi, kateri šport treniraš"* — is deliberate: it invites a comment (which boosts the post) **and** opens a DM-qualification thread (§4.5). The comment is a funnel entrance, not an afterthought.

## 4.4 Discovery / SEO — being found by searching athletes in a tiny market

We have no ad budget and a small audience, so **being findable** is disproportionately valuable — a searchable clip keeps working for years. Iman calls this **minefield / search farming**: durable, low-view, high-intent content that compounds. In a 2.1M market, a Short that ranks for "hitrejši v nogometu" doesn't need a million views — it needs the few hundred *right* Slovenian athletes who search that exact thing every year.

### YouTube (where search actually pays)

Treat YouTube as a search engine, not a feed. For **every Short and every long-form**:

- **Title = the exact query an athlete would type**, in Slovenian, front-loaded with the keyword:
  - Shorts: *"Kako postati hitrejši za nogomet (brez teka na dolge proge)"*, *"Košarka: skočnost v 6 tednih"*, *"Zakaj te split dela počasnega"*.
  - Long-form / VSL: *"ATHLOS — kako trenirati kot profesionalni športnik (slovenski sistem)"*.
- **Description = the search-farmed body.** First two lines repeat the keyword and the promise (that's what shows in search). Then a short paragraph in natural Slovenian using the athlete's own words (from the ICP pains: *"ne vem, ali sploh treniram pravilno"*, *"nihče ne trenira specifično za moj šport"*). End with the **link to the opt-in** and the offer line.
- **Tags:** `nogomet trening`, `košarka trening`, `hitrost`, `eksplozivnost`, `skočnost`, `sprint`, `atletska priprava`, `športna priprava slovenija`, `kondicijski trening`, `Tim Drenovc`, `ATHLOS`.
- **Consistent titling convention** so the channel reads as *the* Slovenian athletic-performance library — the thing that shows up when a coach or athlete searches in Slovenian and finds nothing else serious.

### Instagram (keywords > hashtags now, but use both)

IG search increasingly reads **captions and on-screen text as keywords**, so:
- Put the real query **in the caption and on-screen text** in plain Slovenian ("hitrost", "eksplozivnost", "priprava na sezono", "nogomet", "košarka") — not just in hashtags.
- **Hashtags (3–5, niche not broad):** `#nogomet` `#košarka` `#slovenskišport` `#hitrost` `#eksplozivnost` `#športnitrening` `#kondicija` `#atletskapriprava`. Avoid mega-tags like `#fitness` (2 billion posts, we drown). A tag with tens of thousands of Slovenian posts is where our athlete actually browses.
- **Geo + club language:** name Slovenian places and the sport's own vocabulary (*"za slovenske nogometaše"*, *"pred sezono"*, *"na igrišču"*) so the algorithm and human search both file us under the right, small, high-intent bucket.

### The search-farming mindset (blind-spot honesty)

We are **not** chasing virality — the market is too small and the buyer too specific for a viral hit to convert well (a motivational video with 200K views from Serbia and Croatia sells zero €59 Slovenian subscriptions). We are planting **durable, sport-specific, Slovenian-language answers** to questions our exact buyer types into a search bar. That's the reach lever that survives a founder who can only post a few times a week: it keeps working while Ian builds the app.

## 4.5 Community & DMs — the daily ritual that IS the funnel

At our scale, **engagement is not "support the content" — engagement is the sales team.** Iman automates because he has volume; we do the opposite (per `04` §0): we go **manual where it converts.** At tens-to-low-hundreds of leads, a personal DM out-converts any automated flow. This is Ian's unfair advantage — he can talk to *every single lead* by hand. A big operator can't.

### The daily engagement ritual (30–45 min, every day, posted or not)

Run this every day regardless of whether content went out:

1. **Reply to every comment. Every one.** No exceptions while the audience is small. A reply is free reach, a warmth signal, and often a DM opener. Ask a question back where natural (turns a comment into a thread).
2. **Reply to every DM and every Story reply personally.** No autoresponders. Ian, as himself.
3. **Go outbound, 15 min:** engage genuinely on the posts of Slovenian nogomet/košarka athletes, club pages, and the accounts our ICP already follows (§4.6). Real comments, not "🔥". This is how a cold account gets discovered by the right people — you show up in their world first.
4. **Log warm threads.** Any athlete who replies, comments meaningfully, or opts in → into the Hot/Warm/Cold spreadsheet (from `00-START-HERE.md` lever #1). These are the humans Ian DMs at launch.

### The DM-qualification move (pull a new follower/commenter → waitlist)

When someone new comments, replies to a Story, or follows after a good post, Ian opens a **short, human, non-salesy** thread whose only goal is to hand them the free plan and get an email. Never a wall of text, never a pitch. The move:

**Opener (after they comment/follow):**
> Ej, hvala za komentar! 🙌 Kateri šport treniraš?

**They answer (e.g. "nogomet"):**
> Top. Ravno delam brezplačen 7-dnevni načrt za nogometaše — hitrost + eksplozivnost, napisal ga je Tim (fiziolog). Ti ga pošljem?

**They say yes:**
> Super — vpiši se tukaj [link], da ti ga pošljem takoj: [opt-in]. In povej, kje te trening zdaj najbolj ustavlja — mogoče ti lahko pomagam že prej.

**If they engage but don't opt in:** don't push. Give one piece of genuine free value in the DM (a single cue for their sport), leave the door open. A helped athlete opts in later; a pitched one blocks you. (This is Iman's **disqualification** instinct inverted for warmth — we filter tire-kickers by *not* chasing, and we make the serious ones feel personally coached.)

### Turning followers into fans (parasocial trust)

- **Consistency of face + signature line** (the spoken outro) builds recognition — same person, same promise, every time.
- **Use their words back to them.** When Ian names the exact pain an athlete DM'd him ("ne veš, ali treniraš pravilno") in the next Reel, that athlete becomes a fan — they feel *seen*.
- **Founding-member framing in every interaction:** the early people aren't customers-to-be, they're *the first 100*, the believers who were here before it was public. Say it. It converts followers into evangelists who bring their locker room (§4.6).

## 4.6 Cross-promotion & free distribution — the only paid channel is other people's audiences

We have €0 for ads. So our "media budget" is **other people's trust**: Tim's audience, Slovenian athletes and clubs, other creators, podcasts, and UGC from real users. Iman buys distribution with a clipper army; we *earn* it with collabs and value. This is the highest-ROI growth work available at cold start — one post from a trusted local face outperforms weeks of Ian's cold Reels.

### The free-distribution levers, in priority order

1. **Tim's audience (highest ROI, do this first).** Tim is the science face and has athletes/clients/followers who already trust him. One post/Story from Tim — *"delam na tem, dobi zgodnji dostop + brezplačen načrt"* — is worth more than 20 of Ian's cold Reels (per `04` §2.4). **Lock a minimum Tim content commitment in writing** (this is flagged as a top launch risk in `00-START-HERE.md`): how many minutes/month on camera, and at least one launch-window post to his own audience. Tim as **proof, not promoter** — a short, credible "this is right," not a hype reel.

2. **Slovenian athletes & clubs (collabs + seeding).** Micro-collabs with individual club/academy nogomet & košarka athletes, especially any in Ian's warm graph:
   - **IG Collab Reels** with a local athlete doing a Tim drill → hits both audiences from one post.
   - **Give the app free** to 3–5 credible local athletes in exchange for an honest Story/testimonial as results come in (this also feeds the social-proof engine and the launch emails). Real athlete + real result = the most persuasive content we can make.
   - **Value into club/team group chats and Slovenian FB/Discord communities Ian legitimately belongs to** — drop the free 7-day plan *as value, not spam*. A team sport shares within a locker room naturally; one well-placed drop in a Slovenian nogomet group can out-perform a week of cold Reels.

3. **Other Slovenian creators (co-marketing).** Non-competing adjacent creators — physio/health, sport nutrition, a local strength coach, a football-content creator. Trade a guest appearance, a co-made Reel, or a mutual shout. Each borrows a warm audience for free. Study *their* over-performing formats and reskin them for our niche (Iman: **study other niches, don't copy competitors**).

4. **Podcasts & appearances.** Slovenian sport, fitness, startup, and youth-athlete podcasts / YouTube shows. Ian's angle — *"the founder-athlete building Slovenia's first AI training system, testing it on himself"* — is a genuinely interesting guest story (build-in-public + local + AI). One good 45-minute podcast puts Ian in front of a whole warm audience with high trust transfer, and yields clip fuel for weeks. Pitch 5–10; land 1–2 in the 30 days.

5. **UGC from real users (organic, unpaid).** As founding members get results, make it *easy and rewarding* to share: a founding-member badge, a "postal sem hitrejši z ATHLOS" prompt, a re-share of anything they post. Wire the **Beehiiv referral loop** ("deli s soigralcem → oba dobita bonus protokol") so the locker-room shares happen inside the funnel. This is the honest, unpaid version of Iman's paid-clipper distribution: our users become our clippers because the product actually helped them.

### Blind-spot check on cross-promotion

- **Small market cuts both ways.** Everyone knows everyone — a fake testimonial or a spammy group drop is *permanent* trust death here. Every collab must be genuine, every seeded plan a real gift. Our credibility is the entire currency (from `01` §5).
- **Don't wait for a big audience to start collabs.** Collabs are how a cold account gets its *first* audience — do them in week 1, not "once I'm bigger." Ian's warm graph (lever #1 in `00`) is the seed list for both leads *and* collab partners.
- **Guard the founder's time.** Tim's post, one podcast, and 3–5 athlete seedings will out-produce any amount of extra solo posting. If Ian has two free hours, they go into *one* good collab, not two more Reels.

## Chapter summary — the distribution machine in one page

- **One founder, one camera, one funnel.** Ian is the face (~80% effort); @athlos is a lean proof/offer repository (~20%); short-form is shot once and routed everywhere.
- **The interlock is the point:** spoken outro + pinned comment + IG Collab + Story chain + one link-in-bio move attention **Ian → @athlos → waitlist**, never backward.
- **Cadence has a floor:** daily Ian Story + 3 Reels/3 Shorts from one Content Day + 1 weekly Beehiiv email — the funnel never goes dark; everything above the floor is upside.
- **One Content Day → ~8–10 clips**, re-titled per platform (not re-edited), each carrying an on-screen hook, a spoken outro, and a pinned first-comment CTA — all in native Slovenian.
- **We optimize for being *found* by the right few, not for virality:** search-farmed YouTube titles/descriptions/tags and niche Slovenian IG keywords, because a searchable clip compounds for years while Ian builds the app.
- **Engagement is the sales team:** reply to every comment and DM, run the daily ritual, and use the DM-qualification move to hand new followers the free plan by hand — Ian's unfair advantage over any big operator.
- **Our only "ad budget" is other people's trust:** Tim's audience first, then Slovenian athletes/clubs, other creators, podcasts, and real-user UGC — earned, genuine, and started in week 1.
