# 1 · The Model & The Ground-Up Setup

> This is Chapter 1 of the **ATHLOS Marketing System** — the single operating manual the whole team builds the machine from. Manual text is in English (our working language). Every actual content asset — bios, handles, captions, pinned copy, CTAs — is in native Slovenian, exactly as it will ship. Read this before anyone films, posts, or wires up a single tool.

## 1.1 The model in plain terms

### How the Iman model actually works

Strip away the villa and the screenshots and Iman Gadzhi's marketing runs on four load-bearing ideas. We steal the mechanics, not the persona.

1. **A brand is a machine that turns attention into revenue.** Not a vibe, not a follower count — a *machine* with an input (a stranger's attention) and an output (a paying member). The **Intentional Brand** principle: you map the path from *someone watching* to *someone buying* **before** you make anything. If a piece of content has no path to the offer, you either know it's a nurture piece and post it on purpose, or you don't make it.

2. **Content is a compounding owned asset, not a treadmill.** You are not a content creator posting to survive the algorithm; you are a business owner building "digital real estate" that keeps selling while you sleep. A video filmed once and packaged well keeps working for years. That reframe is what makes the whole thing sustainable for a founder who also has a product to ship.

3. **Optimize for LEADS, not views.** This is the discipline that separates a business from an influencer. A 5,000-follower brand can out-earn a 5,000,000-follower one because it measures the right thing. The first question on every content idea is: *is the goal views, or leads?* View counts are dopamine; leads are money. We ban vanity metrics from our scoreboard (Chapter 1.4 and the metrics section make this concrete).

4. **The founder's face is the non-delegable engine.** People follow a *person*, not a logo they've never heard of. The trust that makes a stranger buy comes from a human being on camera — their story, their opinions, their face. You can delegate editing, scheduling, thumbnails, email setup, analytics, and DMs. You **cannot** delegate being the face. That is the one job that has to stay with the founder.

### How ATHLOS runs it the same way — with Ian as the face

Everything above transfers directly, with one hard adaptation: **we are a cold start** (near-zero audience, a founder who's also shipping the app, a warm list you can count on two hands, a ~2.1M-person Slovenian market, organic-only, ~30 days to MVP). So we invert Iman's default: he optimizes reach-first because he can monetize later at scale; **we optimize warm-list-and-depth first, and treat reach as the slow second engine.** (This blind-spot check runs through the whole manual — never assume Iman's scale.)

**The central mandate: Ian Veber is THE FACE.** Ian films the content, Ian is *in* the content, Ian presents on camera. He is the founder-athlete building the AI training system he wished existed — documenting the whole thing (THE JOURNEY) while training himself on it, in public, in Slovenian. That building-in-public is un-copyable because it's literally his life for the next 30+ days. The entire system in this manual is designed so **Ian can be the on-camera talent while the team builds and runs the machine around him.** Tim Drenovc (physiolog) is the science-credibility face — the proof, not the promoter — appearing lightly where the science needs a real authority behind it.

- **Ian's face → content.** Ian is the reason a stranger stops scrolling. Everything starts with him on camera. The content isn't made "for content's sake" — each piece is reverse-engineered from one call-to-action (the free Tim plan / the waitlist).
- **Content → lead.** The content points at one thing: the free lead magnet. A follower is rented from the algorithm; a **lead is an owned email on the Beehiiv list.** We convert followers into emails as fast as possible, because we own the email and only rent the follow.
- **Lead → nurture.** The Beehiiv list gets a Slovenian story sequence + the core video (VSL). This is where trust in Ian *and* Tim is built. At our scale the warm list is not a footnote — **it is the launch.**

Every step depends on catching leads a single human can still handle by hand.

## 1.2 The non-negotiable principles

These are the rules the whole team operates by. If a decision is unclear, it resolves in favor of these. Print them; they govern every chapter that follows.

1. **Ian is the face. Full stop.** No piece of top-of-funnel content ships without Ian on camera or in voice. We build the machine *around* the founder; we never try to replace him with a logo, a faceless page, or AI voiceover. This is the one non-delegable job. *(Iman: the founder's face is the engine.)*

2. **Leads, not views.** We measure confirmed Beehiiv leads and paying members — never follower count, never Reel views. Anyone who catches themselves celebrating a view count is off-mission. *(Iman: Optimize-for-leads-not-views.)*

3. **Every piece has a path to the offer — or we know it's nurture.** Before we make anything, we map the click-path: content → link-in-bio → free plan → list → offer. If a piece has no path, it's a deliberate nurture/relationship piece for the core, and we label it as such. No "just vibes" posts by accident. *(Iman: Intentional Brand.)*

4. **The warm list is the launch, not a footnote.** The Beehiiv list is the highest-priority asset in the business. We over-index on emailing it, we plug every leak in it, and we give it first crack at the first 100 spots. Reach is the slow second engine; the list is the launch. *(Iman inverted for cold start: No-Leak Backend + warm-list-first.)*

5. **One niche, owned completely, first.** Young Slovenian **football (nogomet) + basketball (košarka)** athletes, 16–26, speed & explosiveness. The enemy is generic bodybuilding splits. We do not say "for all athletes." We earn the right to expand by winning this segment first. *(Iman: Niche-Down-Then-Expand.)*

6. **Manual where it converts, automated where it plumbs.** At a few hundred leads, a personal DM out-converts any automated flow. We automate the plumbing (Beehiiv sequence, Stripe, abandonment, dunning) and we do the *closing* by hand. Ian talks to every lead — that's the unfair advantage a big operator has lost. *(Iman: adapted — he automates because of volume; we go manual because we can.)*

7. **Packaging is 80%.** The big idea + title + thumbnail + hook decide whether content is ever seen. We decide title and thumbnail *before* filming, not after. Thumbnails are engineering (CTR), not art. *(Iman: Packaging = 80%.)*

8. **Scarcity and claims stay honest.** Real, modest scarcity only — "prvih 100, cena zaklenjena za vedno" is true and enough. In a small market where everyone knows everyone, one whiff of a fake counter is permanent trust death. We never fake a number. *(Iman inverted: no scarcity theater.)*

9. **Disqualify openly.** We state clearly who ATHLOS is *not* for. It raises perceived value for free and filters the tire-kickers a solo founder can't afford to babysit in the DMs. *(Iman: Disqualification.)*

10. **Documentation over production.** Ian doesn't need studio gear or to be the world's best trainer (Tim covers the science). He needs to be one step ahead and radically transparent about building ATHLOS. Good audio + clean background + honesty beats a fancy edit. *(Iman: documentation-before-education.)*

## 1.4 The single source of truth — the content board

The whole system runs on **one board.** If it's not on the board, it doesn't exist. This is where an idea becomes a filmed asset becomes a measured result — the machine's dashboard. Build it in Notion (or a shared Trello/Airtable) before Week 1, and give the team edit access so Ian can film while the team moves cards.

### The stages (columns), idea → measured

```
 💡 IDEA ─▶ 📝 SCRIPTED ─▶ 🎥 FILMED ─▶ ✂️ EDITED ─▶ 📅 SCHEDULED ─▶ 🚀 POSTED ─▶ 📊 MEASURED
```

1. **💡 Idea** — a hook + the pillar + the ONE CTA it points to. If it has no CTA path, it's tagged `nurture` on purpose.
2. **📝 Scripted** — the Slovenian hook is written, and the **title + thumbnail concept are decided here** (packaging before filming — principle #7).
3. **🎥 Filmed** — raw footage in `01-raw-footage/`, named per convention. This is the only stage that *needs Ian.* Batched on one Content Day/week.
4. **✂️ Edited** — finished cut in `02-edits/`, caption drafted in Slovenian, thumbnail built. Team-owned, no Ian required.
5. **📅 Scheduled** — loaded into Metricool with date/time, cross-post targets ticked (Ian IG / @athlos IG / YT Short).
6. **🚀 Posted** — live. Link-in-bio and pinned comment both point to the opt-in. Archived to `06-published/`.
7. **📊 Measured** — the only metrics that matter get logged: **leads driven**, saves/shares, watch-through, replies. **Views are noted but never the verdict.** Winners get flagged for future rehashing.

### Card fields (every content card carries these)

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| **Pillar** | THE JOURNEY · Športna znanost (Tim) · Treniram kot športnik · Offer |
| **Account** | Ian · @athlos · both |
| **Format** | Reel/Short · YouTube long-form · Story · Carousel |
| **Goal** | `lead` (growth, points to offer) · `nurture` (relationship, core) |
| **CTA / path** | e.g. „link v biu → brezplačen načrt" |
| **Packaging** | title + thumbnail concept (locked before filming) |
| **Stage** | the 7 columns above |
| **Publish date** | native Slovenian format, e.g. 15. 7. 2026 |
| **Result** | leads driven · watch-through · replies (logged at 📊) |

**The board's non-negotiable rule:** every card must have a **Goal** (`lead` or `nurture`) filled in at the Idea stage. That single field enforces principles #2 and #3 — it forces the team to answer *"is this for leads or views?"* before any effort is spent. A card with no Goal doesn't move to Scripted.

Alongside the content board, keep the **lead log** (the Google Sheet: name / sport / source / pending vs confirmed / replied) and a simple **Hot / Warm / Cold** network sheet. Those two, plus the content board, are the three surfaces the whole system runs on.

## Blind-spot check — the cold start

Before the team executes any of this, internalize where Iman's model quietly assumes a scale we don't have — and what we do instead. **A setup that ignores the cold start is the wrong setup.**

| Iman assumes… | Our reality | So we… |
|---|---|---|
| Millions of views feed the top of the funnel | ~a dozen warm subs, near-zero cold audience | Treat the **warm Beehiiv list as the launch**; reach is the slow second engine, not launch fuel. |
| Enough audience to run many separate accounts + feeder army | One founder also shipping the app; no clipper budget | **Ian's account = ~80% of effort**; @athlos = lean proof repo; cross-post short-form to both from one Content Day. |
| A back-catalog exists to rehash 14×/week | Empty catalog on day 1 | **Rehashing is N/A at launch** — it becomes the #1 anti-burnout lever *after* month 1. Note it, don't lean on it yet. |
| A team of 30 runs the machine | A small team + Ian as the only face | Automate the plumbing, **close by hand**; the board lets the team run everything *except* being on camera. |
| A high-ticket price means a few thousand qualified views = big money | €29–89/mo B2C — **low ticket, needs volume** | We *do* need reach (unlike Iman's business channel), but it must stay **on-niche** (nogomet/košarka speed) and be judged by **leads, not views**. |
| Giant scarcity theater (prize pools, cars) sells | €0 budget, small honest market | **Real, modest scarcity only** — "prvih 100, za vedno." A fake counter here is permanent trust death. |

**The one blind spot to keep front of mind:** Iman can optimize reach-first because he monetizes later at scale. We have 30 days and a handful of believers who can *actually* seed the first 100. So the machine we build in this manual is warm-list-first, founder-face-led, and manual where it converts. Set the success bar to our scale — **5–20 founding members is a win**, not Iman's screenshots.
