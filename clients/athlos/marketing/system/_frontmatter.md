## What this is

This is the single document the whole team builds the marketing machine from. It is not strategy theory — it is a build spec. By the end, every person knows what to set up, what we film, exactly how we film it, how the funnels work, and how the team runs the whole thing week to week.

**The one idea underneath everything:** a personal brand is a *machine that turns attention into revenue.* Ian is the face and the trust engine — that part cannot be outsourced. Everything else (the kit, the edit, the funnels, the ops) the team builds around him so his on-camera job stays small and repeatable. We optimize for **leads, not views.**

**How to read it:** Chapters 1–3 are what you build and film. Chapter 4 is where it goes. Chapter 5 is the money machine behind it. Chapter 6 is how the team runs all of it. Read your chapter deeply; skim the rest so you know how your part connects.

## The locked context (don't re-debate these)

- **Who we sell to first:** young Slovenian football (nogomet) + basketball (košarka) athletes, 16–26, who want to be faster and more explosive. Niche hard — not "all athletes."
- **The offer:** first 100 members lock their price for life. BASIC €29 · PRO ⭐ €59 · ELITE €89 / month.
- **The dual brand:** Ian's personal account = trust engine → the @athlos account = conversion surface → Beehiiv list → Stripe checkout. Organic only, Slovenia-first, MVP launches to the warm list in ~30 days.
- **The honest target:** plan emotionally for **5 founding members, be thrilled at 15.** The real asset we build is a warm list of the right athletes + a working machine + our first testimonials. Do not measure this against anyone's highlight reel.
