# 2 · What To Film — The Content System

This chapter is the filming manual. Chapter 1 set up why Ian is the face and how the machine turns attention into revenue; this chapter answers the single most common question the team will ask every week: **"OK — so what do we actually point the camera at today?"**

Everything here obeys three rules inherited from the foundation docs and the Iman method:

1. **Ian is IN every piece.** No faceless logo content, no stock B-roll standing in for a founder. People follow a person building something, not a fitness app they've never heard of. If Ian isn't on camera or on the mic, it isn't ATHLOS founder content — it's a product asset (fine, but a different job).
2. **Reverse-engineer from the click.** Before anything gets filmed, we know the path: *this video → link in bio → free 7-day Tim plan (Beehiiv) → early-bird cart.* This is Iman's **Intentional Brand** — content is made backwards from the offer, never forwards from "what would get views."
3. **Cold-start honesty.** We are one founder-ish team also shipping the app, a warm list in the dozens, organic-only, ~30 days. So we shoot for **leads and depth**, not raw reach. Every format below is chosen because one person can actually film it this week with a phone.

## 2.1 The content pillars and the 3:1 ratio

### The spine: THE JOURNEY

Every pillar hangs off one spine — **THE JOURNEY: Ian building ATHLOS in public, training himself on it, racing to put it in the hands of the first 100 athletes.** The "dan X/30" countdown is the narrative thread that makes strangers care about a nobody's fitness app: it turns a product launch into a story with a clock on it. When in doubt what to film, film the journey.

### The three pillars (from `01-brand-foundation.md §3`)

| # | Pillar | What it is | What it's FOR | Growth or nurture |
|---|--------|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **1** | **THE JOURNEY — gradim ATHLOS v živo** | Behind-the-scenes of building the app, wins/fails, the "dan X/30" countdown, decisions, the founder's why. | Parasocial trust + waitlist signups. The spine every week returns to. This is what makes the audience feel like *early insiders*, which is exactly what converts them into founding-100 buyers. | **Nurture** (with growth spikes) |
| **2** | **ŠPORTNA ZNANOST z Timom — česar ti nihče ne pove** | Punchy, myth-busting, *sport-specific* performance science: speed, explosiveness, periodization, why generic splits ruin team-sport athletes. Tim is the credibility anchor. | Reach + proof of the science depth behind the app. Highest viral ceiling — "postal sem hitrejši" science travels. This pillar *recruits strangers* and *earns the right to sell*. | **Growth** |
| **3** | **TRENIRAM KOT ŠPORTNIK — dokaz na sebi** | Ian running ATHLOS's own generated programming on himself: the process, the numbers, the speed/explosiveness gains, the grind. | Living proof the product works, embodied. Turns "cool app" into "I want that." Relatability + demonstration in one. | **Growth + proof** |

### The 3:1 growth-to-nurture ratio (Iman's principle, cold-start-adjusted)

Iman's rule: **~3 parts growth, ~1 part nurture.** Growth content is broad, curiosity-driven, brings in strangers — *"makes you known."* Nurture content is lower-reach, higher-trust, deepens the core relationship — *"makes you money."* The universal mistake is stopping at growth and never converting attention into leads.

**For ATHLOS we run roughly 2:1, not 3:1** — and here's the honest reason (per `01 §3` and the funnel doc). At Iman's scale, nurture volume is small relative to a giant funnel, so he can afford 3:1. Ian's warm list is tiny, which makes **each nurture piece disproportionately valuable** — it's where the launch actually converts. So publicly we lean ~2 growth : 1 nurture, and privately we treat the **Beehiiv email list as the highest-priority nurture channel of all** (that lives in Chapter on the funnel — email carries the heaviest nurture load, freeing the feed to lean growth).

Practical weekly mix at ~9–12 pieces:
- **~6 growth** (Pillar 2 science + Pillar 3 proof + hot takes) — the reach engine.
- **~3 nurture** (Pillar 1 journey, founder story, day-in-the-life) — the trust/close engine.
- **Every single piece** ends in the same click-path. Growth pieces use a soft CTA ("link v biu, brezplačen načrt"); nurture pieces can go harder ("prvih 100, ne čakaj").

> **The tag that goes on everything:** before we film, we write two words on the shot list — the **pillar** (1/2/3) and the **job** (growth/nurture). If we can't name the job, we don't shoot it. That discipline *is* the Intentional Brand.

## 2.2 The video-type catalog — the menu Ian films from

This is the concrete format library. When the team plans a Content Day (see §2.4), they pick formats off this menu — not "let's make a video," but "let's make a **myth-bust** and a **founder-trains-on-it proof** and two **listicles**." Each format below gives: what it is, why it works (the Iman framework it leans on), which pillar/job, ideal length, platform, and two ready SL hooks.

> **All hooks are in the first 2 seconds.** Iman: **packaging is 80%** — the hook and the framing decide whether anyone watches. The hooks below are written to be said/shown in the first frame.

---

### FORMAT 1 — Build-in-public "dan X/30"
- **What it is:** Raw phone update on where the build is today. One win, one struggle, one decision. Shot in ~60 seconds, no production.
- **Why it works:** Iman's **documentation-before-education** + the **Intentional Brand** — a countdown creates narrative tension and a reason to come back tomorrow. Strangers who watch "dan 6" feel obligated to see "dan 7."
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 1 · **nurture** (the spine).
- **Length / platform:** 30–60s · IG Reels + YT Shorts (cross-post), Stories.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"Dan 9 od 30. Danes se mi je sesula cela baza vaj — pa sem vseeno bližje kot včeraj."*
  - *"30 dni, da spravim ATHLOS v roke prvih 100 športnikov. Poglej, kje sem danes."*

### FORMAT 2 — Myth-bust
- **What it is:** Name a belief every Slovenian gym kid holds, then demolish it with Tim's science. One myth per video.
- **Why it works:** **Problem Farming** (turns audience pain into an endless backlog) + a **clear enemy** (generic bodybuilding splits). Contrarian hooks earn watch-time; being *against* something is more shareable than being *for* something.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 2 · **growth** (highest viral ceiling).
- **Length / platform:** 30–60s short + can expand to a 6–10 min YT long-form · IG + YT.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"Zakaj te 'klasičen split' z Instagrama dela POČASNEJŠEGA (in kaj delati namesto tega)."*
  - *"Nogometaš, ki dela bench press ponedeljek–petek, dela največjo napako v karieri. Evo zakaj."*

### FORMAT 3 — Tim science cut-in
- **What it is:** Tim on camera for 20–40 seconds explaining ONE protocol or mechanism ("zakaj plyometrija", "kako periodizirati okoli tekem"). Ian frames it; Tim delivers it.
- **Why it works:** **Effortless front, best-in-class backend** — Tim's scarcity *is* his authority. He doesn't run an account or post daily; his rare appearances transfer credibility to Ian and the app. Trust flows Tim → app.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 2 · **growth + proof**.
- **Length / platform:** 20–45s short (or a segment inside a long-form) · IG + YT.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"Vprašal sem fiziologa Tima: zakaj večina športnikov nikoli ne postane hitrejših. Odgovor te bo presenetil."*
  - *"Tim razloži v 40 sekundah, kako pravilno narediti prvi korak eksploziven."*

### FORMAT 4 — Founder-trains-on-it proof
- **What it is:** Ian doing the actual ATHLOS-generated workout — sprint work, plyos, the session the app told him to do today — with the screen shown.
- **Why it works:** **Documentation** + embodied proof. The founder eating his own cooking is the least fakeable content there is. It answers "does this thing even work?" without a single claim.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 3 · **growth + proof**.
- **Length / platform:** 30–60s short · IG + YT; also feeds long-form "teden X rezultati".
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"Aplikacija mi je danes napisala trening. Poglej, kako je videti, ko ga dejansko narediš."*
  - *"30 dni treniram samo po tem, kar generira moja lastna app. Teden 1: to se je zgodilo."*

### FORMAT 5 — Athlete transformation / results
- **What it is:** A real athlete's before/after — sprint time, vertical jump, a number that moved. Early on this is Ian himself; post-launch it's founding members and beta testers.
- **Why it works:** **Optimize for leads not views** — a visible, emotional, *measurable* outcome ("−0,2s na 20m") is the single most persuasive asset for a performance product. Speed is shareable in a way hypertrophy never is.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 3 · **growth + proof** (converts hard).
- **Length / platform:** 30–60s short · IG + YT; testimonial version → sales page.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"Pred 6 tedni je tekel 20m v 3,1s. Danes 2,9s. Nič čarobnega — samo pravi sistem."*
  - *"Ta košarkar je dvignil vertikal za 7cm v enem mesecu. Točno to je delal."*

### FORMAT 6 — "How to" tutorial
- **What it is:** One concrete, do-it-today technique — a drill, a warm-up, a cue. Pure value, screen-recordable or demonstrated.
- **Why it works:** **Minefield/search farming** — high-intent, durable content that compounds and ranks. Someone searching "kako hitreje sprintati" is a *qualified* lead, not a random scroller.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 2 · **growth** (search-durable).
- **Length / platform:** 45–90s short + strong YT long-form candidate · IG + YT.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"3 koraki do bolj eksplozivnega štarta — naredi jih pred vsakim treningom."*
  - *"Kako pravilno narediti drop jump (99% ga dela narobe)."*

### FORMAT 7 — Day-in-the-life
- **What it is:** A day of the founder-athlete: training, building the app, meeting Tim, the grind behind the launch.
- **Why it works:** Iman's classic **nurture** format ("a day in the life" = "makes you money"). Lower reach, high engagement, deep parasocial bond. This is the piece that converts a follower into a *fan*.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 1 · **nurture**.
- **Length / platform:** 60–90s short or 5–8 min YT vlog · IG + YT.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"En dan v življenju, ko gradiš športno app in jo treniraš na sebi hkrati."*
  - *"5:40 zjutraj. 12 dni do lansiranja. Poglej, kako izgleda moj dan."*

### FORMAT 8 — Hot take / opinion
- **What it is:** A strong, defensible opinion about Slovenian sport/fitness culture, said straight to camera. Take a side.
- **Why it works:** **Content trifecta** applied to *what you say* — opinion content is inherently high-watch-time because viewers stay to agree or argue. Establishes Ian's **FOR/AGAINST** positioning (against generic aesthetics culture).
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 2 (culture) · **growth**.
- **Length / platform:** 30–50s short · IG + YT.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"Nepopularno mnenje: 90% slovenskih mladih športnikov trenira, kot da hoče biti bodybuilder — in zato so počasni."*
  - *"Osebni trenerji v Sloveniji so predragi za to, kar dobiš. Zato gradim to."*

### FORMAT 9 — Behind-the-app demo
- **What it is:** Screen recording of ATHLOS doing its thing — the Coach generating a plan, the daily report, the season calendar, biometrija.
- **Why it works:** **Documentation** + proof-of-substance ("*AI, ki dejansko ve nekaj o tvojem športu*", the counter to AI-hype). Shows the product is real before anyone's asked to pay.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 1/product · **nurture + proof**.
- **Length / platform:** 30–60s short · IG + YT; also → the VSL and sales page.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"Vpišem 'nogomet, 17 let, hitrost' — in v 5 sekundah dobim tole. Poglej."*
  - *"To ni še en fitnes tracker. Poglej, kako ATHLOS napiše cel sezonski načrt."*

### FORMAT 10 — Story / origin
- **What it is:** The founder's why. Why Ian is building this, the problem that made him start, the moment it clicked.
- **Why it works:** **Documentation-before-education** + the deepest **nurture** move. Origin stories are the trust-close for fence-sitters — this is the "makes you money" content in its purest form.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 1 · **nurture** (highest-trust).
- **Length / platform:** 60–90s short or a segment of the VSL · IG + YT.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"Nikoli nisem imel strokovne ekipe za sabo. Zato gradim tako, da jo bo imel vsak."*
  - *"Zakaj sem pustil vse ostalo, da zgradim ATHLOS. Iskreno."*

### FORMAT 11 — Listicle ("3 vaje za …")
- **What it is:** A fast numbered list — 3 exercises, 3 mistakes, 3 drills. Snackable, saveable, shareable.
- **Why it works:** **Packaging is 80%** — a number in the hook ("3 vaje") is a proven CTR pattern; listicles get saved and re-watched, which the algorithm rewards. Cheap to batch-film.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 2 · **growth**.
- **Length / platform:** 30–45s short · IG + YT.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"3 vaje za hitrejši prvi korak, ki jih ne dela skoraj nihče."*
  - *"3 napake, zaradi katerih nogometaši ostanejo počasni (delaš vsaj eno)."*

### FORMAT 12 — Reaction / duet
- **What it is:** Ian reacts to a viral Slovenian/Balkan fitness clip or a bad piece of training advice, correcting or building on it.
- **Why it works:** **Study other niches, not competitors** + borrowed reach — reacting to existing content piggybacks on its momentum and positions Ian as the knowledgeable authority. Low effort, high relevance.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 2 · **growth**.
- **Length / platform:** 30–60s short · IG + TikTok (duet-native) + YT.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"Ta 'nasvet za hitrost' ima 200k ogledov in je popolnoma narobe. Popravimo ga."*
  - *"Reagiram na najbolj precenjeno vajo na slovenskem fitnes Instagramu."*

### FORMAT 13 — Testimonial
- **What it is:** A member/beta tester/coach saying, in their words, what changed. First-person, unscripted.
- **Why it works:** **No-leak backend** proof asset + social proof — the highest-converting content type for a paid product. Doubles as a sales-page and launch-email asset.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 3 · **proof / conversion**.
- **Length / platform:** 20–45s short · IG + YT; sales page + launch emails.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"'Prvič v življenju vem, da treniram pravilno.' — Luka, 19, nogomet."*
  - *"Vprašal sem prvega beta uporabnika, kaj bi rekel nekomu, ki okleva. Tole je rekel."*

### FORMAT 14 — POV / relatable
- **What it is:** A "POV:"-framed relatable moment every serious young athlete lives — the loneliness of taking training more seriously than your teammates, the confusion of not knowing if you're doing it right.
- **Why it works:** **Problem Farming** in its most emotional form — relatability content spreads because viewers tag each other ("to si ti"). It surfaces the exact pains the ICP feels (from `01 §2`), which primes them for the solution.
- **Pillar / job:** Pillar 1/2 · **growth** (recruits the exact ICP).
- **Length / platform:** 15–40s short · IG + TikTok + YT.
- **SL hooks:**
  - *"POV: edini v ekipi si, ki trening jemlje resno — in nihče ti ne zna povedati, ali delaš prav."*
  - *"POV: plačaš €40 za osebnega trenerja in ti da isti generični plan kot vsem ostalim."*

**How to read this menu:** Formats 2, 6, 8, 11, 12 are your **growth workhorses** (film many, cheap). Formats 1, 7, 10 are your **nurture spine** (fewer, deeper). Formats 4, 5, 9, 13 are your **proof engine** (they convert — over-index on these as the launch nears). Format 3 (Tim) is your **credibility injector** — sprinkle it across all three jobs.

## 2.3 The core converting video — the Flywheel VSL

This is the **one long-form video** that argues a Slovenian athlete into wanting ATHLOS. Iman's **Flywheel VSL** principle: record ONE evergreen video, once; every other piece links back to it; it compounds into a 24/7 salesman.

### Iman's prerequisite — and Ian's shortcut

Iman says you **cannot** record the VSL cold: you build ~15–30 videos on the micro-channel first *to discover the right topic and angle.* **Ian's cold-start shortcut** (per `04 §5.1` and the `01` blind-spot table): the topic is already known — **it's the launch pitch itself.** We don't need 30 videos to find it. So we build it in Week 2–3, after ~10–15 short pieces have taught us which hooks land, and we fold those winning hooks straight into the VSL. We do **not** burn Week 1 on a VSL no one will see yet.

Right-sized: **aim 8–15 minutes**, not Iman's 38 — cold Slovenian channel, MVP offer, a founder-to-camera piece shot honestly, re-cut after launch.

### The title (SL)

> **"Zakaj generični treningi delajo slovenske športnike počasnejše — in kako to popravim (v živo, na sebi)."**

Alt / thumbnail-line variant: *"Zgradil sem strokovno ekipo, ki jo imajo samo veliki klubi — za ceno dveh treningov na mesec."*

### Beat-by-beat outline

1. **Hook (0:00–0:15) — the enemy, named.** *"Če treniraš nogomet ali košarko in slediš 'splitom' z Instagrama, te ta video reši ene največjih napak v karieri."* Promise + stakes in the first frame. (Packaging = 80%.)
2. **The problem (0:15–2:00) — Problem Farming.** The five ICP pains in their own words (`01 §2`): "ne vem, ali treniram prav", generic English PDFs, no periodization around the season, nobody programs for *your* sport, injuries from unmanaged load. Make the viewer feel *seen*. No pitch yet.
3. **Why it's not your fault (2:00–3:30).** The real cause: elite athletes have a *strokovna ekipa* — physiolog + trener + sezonski planer — that costs €200–500+/month in Slovenia. You've been fighting alone against people who have a team. Reframe: the problem is access, not effort.
4. **The idea (3:30–5:00) — the big reframe.** *"Kaj če bi vsak športnik imel to ekipo — kot AI sistem, ki pozna njegov šport, v slovenščini, za ceno dveh treningov?"* Introduce ATHLOS as the concept, Tim as the science behind it.
5. **Proof it's real (5:00–8:00) — Documentation + demo.** Screen-share the Coach generating a real sport-specific plan live. Cut to Ian doing the session. Cut to a Tim science soundbite. Cut to a number that moved. This is the "it works" block — show, don't claim.
6. **Who it's NOT for (8:00–9:00) — Disqualification.** State plainly: not for shortcut-seekers, aesthetics-only trainers, or people who want someone to train *for* them. This raises perceived value and makes the right-fit viewer feel chosen (`01 §4`).
7. **The offer + why now (9:00–11:00) — Anchoring + honest scarcity.** The €200–500 human-team anchor first, *then* the price: BASIC ~~€49~~ €29 / PRO ⭐ ~~€99~~ €59 / ELITE ~~€149~~ €89. The **first 100 lock the price for life.** Three converging "why now"s: AI is finally good enough, nobody's done this for Slovenian athletes, the door closes at 100.
8. **One CTA (11:00–end) — No-leak.** A single instruction: *"Povezava spodaj. Vpiši se na listo, dobiš brezplačen 7-dnevni Timov načrt — in prvo mesto med prvih 100."* One click-path, nothing else.

### How every other video links back to it

The VSL is the **gravitational center.** Iman's flywheel: every new piece feeds the VSL, the rising traffic gets the algorithm to amplify it, and it compounds ("the older it gets, the more it sells"). Concretely for ATHLOS:

- **Shorts** end with "cela zgodba v enem videu — link v biu / v opisu." The short is the hook; the VSL is the argument.
- **Email N4** ("poglej, kaj sem zgradil") links the VSL for the first time; **L1** (cart open) links it again.
- **Every bio and pinned comment** points to the VSL or the opt-in that follows it.
- **Post-launch**, when the channel has traffic, we run Iman's **content-rehashing** on it (new thumbnail + title only) to keep it climbing — but that's a month-2 lever (empty catalog now).

> **Blind-spot honesty (`04 §5.1`):** at launch the VSL's job is **not** to be found by strangers — it won't get millions of cold views. Its job is to be the trust-and-convert asset Ian links in *every email, every bio, every DM*. It converts the traffic Ian already routes to it. It becomes Iman's evergreen gravity center *later*, as the channel grows.

## 2.4 Short-form vs long-form — and the remix

### What belongs where

| | **Short-form (Reels / Shorts)** | **Long-form (YouTube)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Job** | Reach + top-of-funnel + daily presence. The hook that pulls a stranger in. | Depth + trust + the *convert*. The argument that closes. |
| **Formats** | 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 11, 12, 13, 14 — most of the menu. | The VSL (§2.3); occasional myth-bust or tutorial expanded to 6–10 min; day-in-the-life vlog. |
| **Cadence** | Daily-ish (~1/day, batch-filmed). | ~1 real video (the VSL) for launch; more post-launch. |
| **Platform** | IG Reels + YT Shorts (cross-post), TikTok optional. | YouTube, under **Ian's** channel (the buyer must trust the founder-athlete first). |
| **Cold-start note** | The reach engine, but a *slow trickle* from a cold start — seeds month 2+, doesn't carry launch. | One is enough for launch. Don't run a weekly YT machine yet. |

**Placement rule:** the founder-athlete's *face and daily presence* live in short-form; the *full argument* lives in the one long-form. Product-only assets (pure demos, testimonials) can also live on the @athlos repository account, but the person is always on the personal channel.

### How ONE long-form becomes ~8–10 shorts

Iman's **content-rehashing / repurposing** logic, right-sized. The VSL is shot as one continuous piece but *planned* so each beat is independently clippable. From the 8-beat VSL you extract:

1. The enemy hook (beat 1) → a myth-bust short.
2. Each of 2–3 ICP pains (beat 2) → 2–3 POV/relatable shorts.
3. The €200–500 "strokovna ekipa" reframe (beat 3) → one hot-take short.
4. The live Coach demo (beat 5) → a behind-the-app short.
5. Ian doing the session (beat 5) → a founder-proof short.
6. The Tim soundbite (beat 5) → a Tim science cut-in.
7. The disqualification block (beat 6) → a "ATHLOS ni za …" short.
8. The offer/why-now (beat 7) → a launch/scarcity short (held for launch week).

That's **~9 shorts from one shoot** — one Content Day yields a week-plus of feed.

> **Cold-start reality (from `00-START-HERE` §"Cut or defer"):** do **not** treat the 8–10-shorts math as a mandate this month — "nobody's watching enough yet." **Post the clips that are genuinely good, ignore the ones that aren't.** The remix engine is real but its full value arrives in month 2+ when there's an audience to catch the volume. For launch: one Content Day/week, cut 3 good shorts *if* it makes anything, and let the VSL do the heavy converting.

### The Content Day (Iman's batch principle)

Batch-film one day a week (Iman: "Content Day" — 2–5 pieces in one sitting). Good audio + a clean background beats fancy gear. One session: shoot the day's journey update, 2–3 myth-busts/tutorials, one Tim cut-in if Tim's there, one founder-training clip. Everything else that week is raw phone documentation (Format 1), which needs no setup.

## Blind-spot check

Where this chapter leans on Iman and where the cold start forces a change — so the team never over-applies a framework built for scale:

- **3:1 → 2:1 ratio.** Iman's 3:1 assumes a big funnel where nurture is a small slice. Ian's warm list is tiny, so nurture is disproportionately valuable — we run ~2:1 in the feed and let **email carry the heaviest nurture load**. Don't starve the trust engine chasing reach.
- **The remix engine is deferred, not deleted.** "One long-form → 8–10 shorts" is a real system, but at cold start nobody's watching enough to justify the volume. Post the *good* clips, ignore the math this month (`00-START-HERE`). It becomes a core lever in month 2+.
- **The VSL doesn't get found — it gets sent.** Iman's VSL works on millions of feeder views. Ian's won't get cold reach at launch. Its job is to convert the traffic Ian *already routes to it* via emails, bios, and DMs. Build it once, let it compound later.
- **Content-rehashing is N/A now.** No back-catalog exists to repackage. Note it as Ian's #1 anti-burnout lever *after* month 1 — don't try to use it in Week 1.
- **The topic-discovery prerequisite is shortcut, not skipped.** Iman needs 15–30 videos to find the VSL angle; Ian's angle is the launch pitch itself, so ~10–15 shorts to *validate hooks* is enough. But we still validate before we film the VSL — we don't shoot it blind on Day 1.
- **Every format assumes one person with a phone.** No format in the catalog requires a crew, a studio, or a paid clipper army (Iman's Content Rewards is off the table, €0 budget). If a format can't be shot by Ian + Tim on a Content Day, it's not in the bank.
- **Reach stays on-niche.** Unlike Iman's business channel (deliberately small, high-ticket), ATHLOS is low-ticket B2C and *does* need volume — but volume of the *right* people. A viral generic-motivation clip that pulls in non-athletes is a failure, not a win. Judge every piece by **waitlist signups**, not view count (`04 §7`).
